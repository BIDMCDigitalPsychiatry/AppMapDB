Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Stay consistent with your medications, never miss a dose, build healthy routines, and take control of your health—one day at a time. MyTherapy is your digital health companion, reminding you to take your medications on time, supporting your personalized medication plan, and helping you track your health in a clear and structured way. All your health routines—from medications to measurements—in one simple app. Because consistency is key to taking care of yourself.

MyTherapy is a medication reminder and pill tracker app that supports your medication management, helps you track your health, and keeps you on top of your treatment.

Key features at a glance:

● Pill Reminder & Medication Alarm: Set flexible, personalized reminders for pills, medications, and treatments—reliable and easy to adjust to your routine.

● Personal Medication Plan: Keep a clear overview of your medications, including a daily log to track what you’ve taken.

● Refill & Prescription Reminders: Stay prepared—get notified before your medication runs out or when it’s time for your next prescription.

● Injection Site Tracking: Easily document and rotate injection sites to support safe and effective treatment.

● Blood Pressure & Weight Tracking: Get reminders for more than 20 health measurements and record them directly in MyTherapy. Track your weight over time with built‑in weight loss support to help you reach and maintain your health goals.
