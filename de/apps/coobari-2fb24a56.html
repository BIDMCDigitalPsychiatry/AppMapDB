A SPACE TO PAUSE, REFLECT AND GROW

Coobari is a mindset and wellbeing app designed to help kids and teens understand themselves, navigate their emotions and relationships, and build skills for everyday life.

Growing up brings many different thoughts, feelings, experiences and changes. Coobari gives young people a supportive space to explore what is happening within them, discover what matters to them and find tools that work for who they are.

Whether they need a moment to reset, want to make sense of how they feel or are learning to navigate an everyday situation, Coobari helps young people pause, reflect and grow.

PAUSE

When emotions feel overwhelming or a busy mind needs a reset, young people can explore simple mindfulness activities and guided tools to help them slow down, reconnect with the present moment and find their calm.

REFLECT

Through journalling, thoughtful prompts and guided activities, young people can explore their thoughts, feelings, values, interests and experiences. Reflection helps them build self-awareness and discover more about who they are.

GROW

Practical stories, videos, animations and activities help young people develop skills for emotions, confidence, friendships, communication, learning and everyday life. Coobari encourages growth in a way that feels personal, supportive and relevant.
