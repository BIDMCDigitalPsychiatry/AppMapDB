Quabble: Daily Mental Health

Ease anxiety & lift mood

Reduce stress, anxiety, and depression with joyful daily self-care routines. Quabble offers a wide range of therapist-backed mental health practices to support your well-being. With your Quabble Duck by your side, caring for your mental health becomes a natural and uplifting part of every day.

All-in-One Mental Health App

Developed by people who understand real-life challenges, Quabble brings evidence-based practices and mindfulness training into one easy-to-use app. No more juggling multiple apps for meditation, journaling, sleep, or emotional support, Quabble helps you manage it all in one calming place.

What You’ll Find Inside

Therapist-Backed Workouts

Practice CBT-inspired grounding, positive affirmations, gratitude reflection, and mindfulness meditation to strengthen six key areas of wellness: Emotional Regulation, Physical Health, Sleep, Mindfulness, Social Support, and Personal Growth.

Daily Mini-Routines & Habit Tracker

Start mornings with intention and end with reflection. The First Bloom Journey 15-day program helps you build sustainable self-care habits, track progress, and celebrate growth with small rewards.
