Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Sober Living App is a FREE Personal Recovery Record that can help track and share your journey of recovery.

Simply speaking, this A.A. based app is a digital notebook intended to be useful regardless of type or duration of your addictions.

Here you will find seamless ways to -

• track your sobriety streaks and get stunning anniversary coins

• focus on goals + milestones one day at a time

• ask Sober AI for guidance

• connect with real life sponsors and accountability partners

• find and log group meetings and events

• adherence logs for those who need to know

• personalize a 12 step program that works for you

• plan out how, when, and to whom to make amends

• document + follow your spiritual progress

• celebrate and share anniversaries

• pray + meditate whenever you seek solace

• set reminders for appointments and medications

• and much more

Sober Living app is based upon the enduring principles of Alcoholic Anonymous but has no affiliation with any fellowship program. Our purpose is to strengthen connections with recovery programs and sponsors of your choosing. Adherence logs can be automated for those under formal recovery mandates.

Specifically, Sober Living app has the tools to help you throughout your recovery journey -

* ChatGPT to answer your quesitions

• follow multiple cravings or addictions

• re-set streaks and learn from the past

• use wide range of helpful menus and templates

• sign into A.A. Meetings online

• seek valuable insights…
