* Relaxing Dark Mode

Noise Machine is the best noise maker app! The beautiful design of this white noise maker app is a joy to use each night.

Noise Machine features four “colors” of noise - each with different properties:

• Brown Noise (also known as Red Noise) has a rich, deeper character with strong low frequencies and roars like a river or an ocean. Brown noise is beneficial for sleep, and even more effective when played through a powerful speaker to bring out its deep tones. Our brown noise is mathematically accurate, representing the true nature of brown noise.

• Green Noise has an ambient and natural feel that gives the feeling of being in a forest or river valley. Green noise is energizing like a long walk in the woods.

• Pink Noise has a more balanced texture that highlights the middle frequencies evenly, like a calming rain storm. Our pink noise is mathematically balanced.
