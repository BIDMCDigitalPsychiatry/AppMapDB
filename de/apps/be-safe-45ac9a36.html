Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

The Be Safe app is free and enables the person with thoughts of suicide to create a safety plan that will help keep them safe should another suicidal crisis occur. It is a person driven app that empowers them to manage their thoughts/behaviours.

Phone contacts and Helplines can be called directly from the app without scrolling through their contact list. When they have completed their safety plan, they can email it directly from the app to formal or informal supports of their choice.

And their safety plan can be updated at any time.This app is based on the latest research by Barbara Stanley and Gregory Brown. You can read more about the authors by clicking on this link: https://suicidesafetyplan.com
