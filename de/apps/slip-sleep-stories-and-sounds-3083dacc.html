Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Some nights, your mind just won't switch off. Slip is here for those nights.

We made Slip to be the gentlest possible landing into sleep — a place where bedtime stories, calming music, nature sounds, and quiet ASMR all live together in one cozy app. No pressure, no streaks, no tracking. Just press play, settle in, and let go.

A library of sleep stories that actually help you sleep

From timeless folktales to soft, drifting modern tales, every story in Slip is written and narrated to slow your thoughts down. Plots that don't pull you forward. Voices that don't pull you back. Stories built to be unfinished — because the goal isn't to hear the ending. It's to miss it.

Voices you'll want to fall asleep to

Slip features a range of narrators, so you can find the voice that suits you — softer or deeper, brighter or more hushed. Some nights you'll want one. Some nights another. They're all waiting.

Calming music, nature sounds, and ASMR

Piano pieces from the great composers. Rain on a tin roof. Crackling fires, distant thunder, ocean tides. Soft-spoken whispers and gentle tapping for ASMR listeners. Mix and match, or let one quiet sound carry you through the night.

Offline listening, anywhere

Download your favorite stories and sounds and bring them with you — on flights, on trains, to the cabin, to bed. No signal needed. No interruptions.

A space that feels calm to open

Slip's design is deliberately soft and uncluttered. Opening the app at midnight shouldn't wake you up.
