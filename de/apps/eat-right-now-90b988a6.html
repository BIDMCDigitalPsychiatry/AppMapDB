Struggling with binge eating, emotional eating, or stress eating? Eat Right Now® helps you break free from food cravings and build mindful eating habits that last—without dieting, restriction, or guilt.

Backed by neuroscience and mindfulness, Eat Right Now is clinically proven to reduce craving-related eating by 40%*. The program helps you retrain your brain so you can stop overeating, manage food cravings in real-time, and finally feel at ease in your relationship with food.

Whether your goal is weight loss, better health, or freedom from overeating, Eat Right Now gives you the tools, lessons, and support you need to change your eating habits for good.

Join Eat Right Now to…

- Identify what triggers emotional and stress eating habits

- Practice mindful eating techniques to enjoy food without guilt

- Reduce craving-related eating one step at a time

- Build long-term habits that support weight loss and health naturally

With Eat Right Now, you will receive:

- Daily guided lessons to learn at your own pace

- Mindfulness exercises, theme weeks, and bonus content

- Interactive tools to work with food cravings in real-time

- 24/7 moderated community and journal

- Live weekly sessions with our team of behavior change experts

- Optional weight management modules

- Customizable daily goals and reminders
