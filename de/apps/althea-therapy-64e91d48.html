Mental health is a journey, and we believe that every individual deserves a compassionate and culturally responsive guide.

At Althea Therapy, our mission is to destigmatize therapy and improve mental health outcomes for historically marginalized communities. We recognize the profound impact that race, culture, and systemic barriers can have on one's well-being, and we are committed to providing a safe space for healing and growth.

How Althea Therapy Works

Culturally Responsive Therapy: Connect with licensed counselors, therapists, and wellness professionals who understand the nuances of your cultural background and lived experiences.

Unlimited Private Messaging: Access unlimited private messaging with your therapist, ensuring seamless and convenient communication.

Comprehensive Resources: Explore our free guides, workbooks, personal journals, and mood trackers designed to support your mental health journey.

Therapist-Led Group Experiences: Join our online group learning experiences facilitated by experienced therapists, fostering a sense of community and shared understanding.

Wellness Events and Resources: Discover and participate in events, workshops, job opportunities, and wellness information tailored to your needs.
