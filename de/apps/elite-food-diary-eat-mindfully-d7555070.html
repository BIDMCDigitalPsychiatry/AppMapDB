Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Your day, one meal at a time. Elite is a visual food diary for a more mindful daily routine.

Save a photo or a short meal description. Get an AI calorie estimate, review it, or enter your own value. Explore your entries as bubbles or a list, choose a daily calorie goal, connect with friends, and use reminders that adapt to your diary. Calorie estimates are approximate.

After introductory access, continued meal logging requires Premium. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically. Prices and renewal terms are shown before purchase. Manage or cancel your subscription in App Store settings.
