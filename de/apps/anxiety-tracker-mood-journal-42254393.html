Anxiety Log: Manage Your Mental Health

Take charge of your emotional well-being with Anxiety Log – the ultimate companion for understanding and managing anxiety, stress, and panic attacks. Designed to offer valuable insights into your mental health, this app is your personal tool for empowerment and self-care.

**Key Features**

✅ Daily Anxiety Check-Ins

Monitor your anxiety levels and mood fluctuations with daily check-ins using the GAD-7 test. Track changes over time and gain deeper insights into your mental state.

✅ Panic Attack Log

Record symptoms and triggers of panic attacks to anticipate and prevent future episodes. Enhance your awareness and avoid potential triggers for a more balanced life.

✅ Positivity Journal

Cultivate a positive mindset and gratitude with the free journaling feature. Document moments of joy and appreciation to uplift your mood and mental well-being.

✅ Comprehensive Data Analysis

Utilize powerful analysis tools to evaluate your anxiety, gratitude, panic attack occurrences, and medication usage. Gain valuable insights for more effective mental health management.

✅ Secure and Private

Rest assured that your personal data is secure and confidential with Anxiety Log. No account is required to use the app, and we prioritize your privacy and protect your sensitive information!
