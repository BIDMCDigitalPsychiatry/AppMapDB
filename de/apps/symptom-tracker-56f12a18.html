Aus dem App-Store

Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Record health events and symptoms for yourself and your loved ones throughout the day in our easy-to-use health and symptom tracker app. Arm yourself and your doctor with the most accurate and complete info possible.

* New! Customize your events to track your specific events, conditions, and illnesses. Track your periods, ovulation, chronic illnesses, and more.

* Track respiratory symptoms, fever, aches & pains, ear/nose/throat issues, eye issues, digestive issues, and skin symptoms in real time.

* Using a pulse oximeter to track blood oxygen level? Log your readings to share with your doctor.

* Track each time a medicine is taken. You can manage the list of medications available for each person you're tracking for.

* Add photos where needed, such as for eye or skin inflammation or rashes.

* Add yourself and any loved ones you want. Track for your whole family, your roommate - anyone. You can even give others access to log for individuals on your account.

* Multiple methods to share data with your doctor.
