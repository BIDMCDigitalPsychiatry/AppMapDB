FoodMate Will Help You

- Get rid of overeating and snacking

- Assuage your guilt about food and body image

- Learn not to lose control while eating something yummy

- Realize why you can’t stop eating even when you’re full

Food Diary

- Define the habits that prevent your weight stabilization

- Show how food, emotions, and overeating are interrelated

- Develop a strategy for improving your eating habits

- Help control nutrition in critical situations

10 DAYS Intuitive Eating Challenge

- Discover the habits that prevent your weight stabilization

- Create a flexible meal schedule

- Recognize hunger and stop eating non stop

- Learn to choose products, buy them, and eat intuitively

10 modules with theory and practice, 30-40 minutes each.

Take them at any time. The results will stay with you forever.

The Practice Section Will

- Help you choose products, save recipes, and make intuitive eating plans

- Examine your schedule and timely remind you to check your hunger

- Teach you to follow mindful eating and cope with reasons for overeating

- Ease your anxiety about food
