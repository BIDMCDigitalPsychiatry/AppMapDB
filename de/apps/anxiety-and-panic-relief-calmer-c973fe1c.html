Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Calmer is a toolkit for anxious, stressful moments: quick ways to calm down, and small daily habits that help you feel steadier over time.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• SOS calm-down tools for moments of panic

• Guided breathing, grounding and relaxation exercises

• Sleep stories and guided meditations to help you wind down

• Anxiety School: short lessons on how stress and anxiety work

• A daily plan and a guided 7-session program

• Mood check-ins to notice what helps you

• AI companion: talk things through any time (not a therapist or medical service)

WHY PEOPLE USE CALMER

• Calm down in minutes, wherever you are

• Build a simple routine that fits your day

• Understand what sets off your stress

• Get to sleep with less racing thought

IMPORTANT

Calmer is a self-help wellness app. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for professional care. If you're struggling, please talk to a doctor or a mental health professional. If you're in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line; in the app, tap the help button in the AI companion.

SUBSCRIPTION

Calmer Plus unlocks all exercises, stories, lessons and unlimited AI companion messages. Monthly and yearly plans are available; some include a free trial.
