Blood pressure pro is an app that allows users to record and monitor blood pressure

Aus dem App-Store

Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

We present an application which allows you easy recording and monitoring blood pressure. We prepared it in close cooperation with patients and paramedics to provide you best functionality. We developed the application to be elegant, complement the appearance of the system, and to be as intuitive and ergonomic as possible. All buttons are positioned so that they can be easily operated with your thumb.

You can access the data in the calendar view as well as the app allows you to observe results on statistics. If you measure your blood pressure the regular testing is very important. The app has built-in reminding system. It is possible to set multiple alarms.

We also designed a special high blood pressure indicator based on symbols and colors. The indicator messages are based on the guidelines of the American Heart Association, Inc. It is also possible to provide your own hypertension configuration for the indicator.

All the data are shareable. You can export results to printable PDF file. It is also possible to export data to CSV file, which is easy to open in every spreadsheet application.

The application is a tool to support you in daily measurement. It cannot be taken as medical advice. The diagnosis always must be provided by qualified medical service.
