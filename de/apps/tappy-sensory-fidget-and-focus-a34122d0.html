Welcome to Tappy, The Ultimate Sensory Fidget & Wellness Companion.

Tappy is a feature-packed self-care fidget app designed to help you de-stress, unwind, and refocus in a fun and satisfying way! As you tap or slide, you will enjoy incredible haptic responses and sound effects. And there is also so much more to Tappy! Discover new ways to stay mindful, boost your mood, and find your daily calm. You'll find great fidget games and tools (like a Fidget Spinner!) that give satisfying haptic feedback responses, as well as guided meditations, soundscapes, breathing exercises, mood tracking, journaling, and habit tracking! Tappy aims to be your one-stop self-care fidget app for relaxing, focusing, and just having some fun!

Tappy (Energy Bar and Actions):

• Tap on Tappy to boost your count!

• Tap the Actions button to show a list of Actions you can do with Tappy such as Sleep, Send Love, Celebrate, and Start the Focus Timer!

• Tappy energy bar at the top of the Tap tab: Throughout the day this energy bar with lower, keep Tappy nice and energized by tapping on him to increase his energy!

• Change the haptic feedback type by tapping the customizer.

• In the customizer there are multiple locked features. Press these locks to see how many taps you need to unlock it!
