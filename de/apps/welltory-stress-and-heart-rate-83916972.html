Welltory connects with user's apple health app to provide passive health data, such as heart rate. Users can input their current symptoms and mood, and an AI chatbot will respond with treatment advice. With a premium subscription, users can access more personalized recommendations. Health data is summarized into daily, weekly and monthly graphs.
