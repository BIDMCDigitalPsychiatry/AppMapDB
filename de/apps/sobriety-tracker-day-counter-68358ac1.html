Overcome addiction and stay sober with the Sobriety Tracker Counter app — your daily companion for addiction recovery, habit tracking, and progress monitoring.

Quitting any addiction — whether it’s smoking, alcohol, or unhealthy habits — is challenging, but tracking your progress makes it easier. The Sobriety Tracker Counter app helps you monitor how long you've been addiction-free, stay focused on your goals, and celebrate every sober milestone.

Looking for a simple and effective addiction recovery tracker? Need a reliable sobriety tracker to stay motivated? Want to build healthy habits and quit smoking, drinking, or other addictions? This is the app for you.

Track addictions easily:

Whether you're trying to quit smoking, stop drinking, or break other bad habits, this app helps you log your progress, track your sober days, and visualize your addiction-free timeline.

Daily milestone reminders & motivational messages:

Receive daily notifications to help you stay strong and focused on your addiction recovery journey. Celebrate each day you stay sober, and get encouragement right when you need it.

Plan your new routine:

Use the calendar and habit scheduler to plan healthy habits and stick to them. Replace old addictions with positive, healthy routines.
