Ready to quit smoking for good?

Qwit is a powerful quit smoking app designed to help you stop smoking, manage cravings, and stay nicotine-free – one day at a time.

Join over 1,000,000+ people worldwide who have already started their smoke-free journey with Qwit.

Whether you want to quit cigarettes, vaping, or reduce nicotine progressively, Qwit gives you practical tools, clear motivation, and real-life tracking to stay in control.

🚭 What makes Qwit different

Qwit focuses on what actually helps when quitting smoking:

• Tracking real cravings and relapse moments

• Managing nicotine substitutes (patches, gum, lozenges, vape, etc.)

• Immediate help when the urge hits

• Clear progress, visible rewards, and motivation that grows with you

🔥 Key features

• Craving & relapse tracker – Log cravings, slip-ups, and triggers to better understand your habits

• Emergency craving help – Immediate support when you feel the urge to smoke

• Nicotine substitute tracking – Follow patches, gums, sprays, or vaping reduction

• Smart quit coach (AI-powered) – Personalized advice based on your quit date, habits, and progress

• Detailed statistics – Cigarettes not smoked, money saved, time smoke-free, health progress

• Rewards & achievements – Turn your savings into rewards and milestones

• Widgets – Track your progress directly from your home screen

• Customization – Set your goals, reminders, and motivation style

• Multilingual – Available in 20+ languages
