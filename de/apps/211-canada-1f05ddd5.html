Don’t know where to go for help?

Find community services near you with ease using the interactive map search feature. Locate essential services such as healthcare, food assistance, housing, and more.

Create a personalized list of your most-used services for quick access whenever you need them.

Connect directly with service providers via calls or chats, making it easier than ever to get the assistance you require.

About 211

211 is Canada’s primary source of information for government and community-based, mental and non-clinical health and social services.

211 is available by phone, chat, website, and text in different regions – dial 2-1-1 to connect to community services.

You do not have to give your name or personal details to get information.
