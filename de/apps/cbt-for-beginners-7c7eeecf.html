Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

CBT Therapy: Mental Health Journal is a structured, science-based tool designed to help you reduce overthinking, manage anxiety, and develop healthier thinking patterns. Based on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), the app guides you through proven techniques that support emotional regulation, clearer thinking, and long-term mental wellness.

This journal is built for users who want a practical, evidence-based approach to understanding and improving their thoughts, emotions, and daily reactions.

What the app offers

Clear and organized thought processing

Write freely or use structured CBT prompts to analyze situations, challenge distortions, reframe negative thoughts, and build more balanced perspectives.

Tracking emotional progress

Monitor your mood, thought patterns, progress indicators, and long-term trends. Understand how your mindset changes over time.

Science-based CBT techniques

Access a library of practical tools, including thought reframing, anxiety reduction exercises, grounding strategies, stress-management techniques, and cognitive restructuring.

Personalized daily plan

Follow a structured, goal-oriented plan built around your needs, whether you aim to reduce overthinking, manage anxiety, improve self-awareness, or strengthen emotional resilience.

AI-generated suggestions

Receive personalized, context-aware recommendations based on your journal entries. AI provides practical next steps rooted in CBT principles.
