As humans, we’re hardwired for connection. Belonging to a community makes us feel safe and helps us thrive.

But so often, living with chronic migraine can make you feel physically and emotionally isolated. Not only can it be hard to do things you loved before your diagnosis, but it can also feel like no one understands what it’s like.

Until now.

Our mission is to cultivate a space powered by the migraine community and empowered by each other. From one-to-one chats to conversation forums, we make connecting easy. This is a safe place to find and receive advice, to seek and offer support, and to discover the authentic stories of members, just like you.

Bezzy Migraine is a free online platform bringing new meaning to the word “community.”

Our aim is to create an experience where:

- everyone feels seen, valued, and understood

- everyone’s story matters

- shared vulnerability is the name of the game

Bezzy Migraine is a place where you are more than your migraine. It’s a place where, finally, you belong.

HOW IT WORKS

Social-first Content

Like all of your favorite social networks, we’ve designed an activity feed to connect you with other members living with chronic migraine. We pride ourselves on making Bezzy Migraine a safe and secure space where you can join live discussions, connect one-to-one, and read the latest articles and personal stories.
