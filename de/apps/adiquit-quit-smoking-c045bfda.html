Would you like to quit smoking for good?

Then Adiquit is the right choice!

Adiquit is the only app based on scientific knowledge and real clinical practice. A virtual therapist provides professional guidance during the quitting process and helps smokers to overcome withdrawal symptoms.

Adiquit has been created by an international team of leading experts on addiction treatment from Europe and the USA. The app is based on a complex professional support of smokers that is commonly used in therapist practice.

Clinical studies show the chance of quitting successfully with Adiquit is as much as six times greater compared to quitting without any professional help. You’re saving your time using Adiquit - there is a professional support always at hand.

Smoking is an addiction and quitting is harsh. Majority of unsuccessful attempts are caused by the lack of information on the principles of addiction and insufficient preparation. Thorough preparation and professional support are therefore crucial during quitting.

Adiquit is a cigarette addiction specialist, however, it also covers other tobacco and nicotine products.

How does Adiquit work and why does it reach such amazing results?

It adapts the therapy to meet your individual needs.

The programme starts with a ten-days preparation, then six weeks long quitting therapy follows.

There is an everyday short dialogue with the virtual therapist.

It supports you at the beginning but also during your quitting.
