Rosebud is your personal AI-powered self care companion. Rosebud is a therapist-recommended journaling and self-reflection tool designed to support your personal growth and emotional wellness. Rosebud is a diary that evolves with you, learning from your entries and providing personalized prompts, feedback, and insights tailored for your growth.

THE BEST DAILY JOURNALING APP

Navigating challenging emotions? Want to better manage stress, anxiety, or overthinking? Rosebud is designed to help you work through difficult emotions and thoughts through structured self-reflection. Whether you prefer to write or speak your thoughts, with just a few minutes of voice or text journaling, you'll reduce stress and gain clarity.
