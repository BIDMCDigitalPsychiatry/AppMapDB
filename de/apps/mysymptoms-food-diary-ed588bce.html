Rezension des Bewerters

Diese Rezension gibt die Meinung der bewertenden Person wieder und stellt keine Empfehlung durch MIND dar.

mySymptoms is a non-specific health app providing support for conditions such as eczema, asthma and chronic stomach pain. The app allows users to track their daily symptoms, summarizing the data into 1-month, 3-month, 6-month and 12-month graphs of symptom intensity.

Explore the connection between your diet and health with mySymptoms. Easily track your meals, symptoms, and share detailed journals with your clinicians. Our sophisticated analysis highlights patterns and associations between your dietary habits and symptoms, empowering you with the insights needed to support your digestive health journey.

Try mySymptoms free for 7 days. The app is subscription-based and includes food and symptom tracking, plus analysis to uncover patterns that support your health journey.

Featured in ‘The Best Gut Health Apps’ by Healthline. Join over 1 Million people who use mySymptoms for their IBS, IBD, low FODMAP diet, migraines, eczema, and more.

“This app is simply the best in terms of keeping track of your foods and symptoms. In addition to medication and exercise. It is helping me a lot to keep a journal to track my SIBO and IBS.” [mySymptoms customer]

DIARY / JOURNAL

• Food & health tracking: Log all aspects of your health, including food, drink, medication, stress, exercise, and environmental factors.

• Food database: Search or scan barcodes to access our extensive database of foods.

• Symptom tracking: Log symptom intensity, duration, and additional notes.

• Wellness Metrics: Log your energy, sleep quality, and bowel movements (using Bristol scale)

• Customization: Add your own drinks, foods, meals, medications, exercises, and symptoms.
