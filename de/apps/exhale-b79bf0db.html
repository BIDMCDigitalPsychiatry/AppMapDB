No more waiting to Exhale!

As Black Women, we navigate systems of oppression that take a toll on our minds, bodies, and souls. Releasing stress and returning to our breath is essential to healthier, more balanced lives.

Exhale is your haven—a place of rest and rejuvenation. This app is an inclusive resource that centers Black Women, offering a space you can trust, designed for your emotional well-being journey. While Exhale is created with Black Women in mind, we provide resources for all who seek healing, balance, and renewal. We hope Exhale becomes a refuge you can return to as often as you need for restoration and peace.

Key features include:

Meditations/Guided Journey

Breath Work Techniques

Calming Sounds

Thought of the Day

Daily Push Notifications

Animated Breathing Orb

Ready to Exhale the Podcast

Topics Include:

Rest

Embodiment

Reduce Stress

Inner Peace

Ancestors

Grounding

Calming Sounds

Microaggressions
