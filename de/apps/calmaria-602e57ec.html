Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Calmaria — meaning calmness in Portuguese — returns with its biggest update yet. Version 3.0 introduces HealthKit integration and seamless sync between iPhone and Apple Watch, keeping your breathing sessions and progress perfectly aligned across devices.

Designed to bring moments of stillness into your day, Calmaria helps you practice mindful breathing through exercises like 4-7-8, Box, 7-11, and 5-0-5-0. Personalize your experience with new themes, track your journey with detailed statistics, and let Calmaria guide you toward better focus, relaxation, and balance.

Whether you’re just beginning or refining your breathing practice, Calmaria 3.0 is your pocket companion for a calmer, clearer mind — now smarter, smoother, and more connected than ever.
