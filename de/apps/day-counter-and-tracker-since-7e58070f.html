Rezension des Bewerters

Diese Rezension gibt die Meinung der bewertenden Person wieder und stellt keine Empfehlung durch MIND dar.

Since (Day Counter & Tracker) is a non-specific app allowing users to quit bad habits, form good habits, celebrate happy days or commemorate sad ones. The app also allows users to track other routines, such as medication or errands. Users are provided with weekly, monthly and yearly data (paid subscription is required to see the yearly data) which are summarized through a standard bar graph.
