Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Steadyline is a bipolar mood tracker and symptom journal for recording low and elevated moods separately. Review your patterns over time and prepare a clear report for your psychiatrist.

Keep a daily record of how you feel and what happened between appointments, with charts that help you look back across weeks and months.

DAILY BIPOLAR TRACKING

• Record low mood, elevated mood, anxiety, irritability, and energy.

• Add symptoms, triggers, changes in movement or activity, and daily notes.

• Track sleep duration and quality alongside your mood.

• Log medication adherence and set reminders.

• Include optional menstrual-cycle context.

• Use Voice Log when typing feels difficult.

REVIEW YOUR MOOD PATTERNS

See your entries together in charts. Current Signals highlights patterns in the data you record, while the Stability Score summarizes recent self-reported trends. Review sleep and medication records alongside mood changes for additional context.

PREPARE FOR PSYCHIATRIST APPOINTMENTS

Create a structured report with mood trends, sleep patterns, medication adherence, symptoms, and stability. Protect your report with a password and share it with your psychiatrist or doctor when you choose.

OPTIONAL AI REFLECTION

Ask SAM can help you review saved logs and summarize patterns for reflection. AI features are optional and can be turned off.
