Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

WEconnect is a versatile mobile app that empowers individuals on their journey towards mental wellness. This tool is designed to assist those dealing with various behavioral health concerns, including anxiety, grief, substance use disorders (SUD), mental health issues, and other challenges that affect quality of life.

Using the WEconnect app you can

- Meet 1 on 1 with a dedicated peer support specialist

- Attend online support meetings

- Track your goals and create healthy habits

- Celebrate your progress

- Stay on track with your recovery

* note: peer support is only available to those who are signed up through their employer or insurance provider

Get the app to join online support meetings instantly!

WEconnect has been crafted by individuals who possess deep, personal insights into the challenges of maintaining mental and emotional health. We know everyone’s journey is different. That’s why we made the app: to help people improve their lives.

With multiple meetings hosted every day, WEconnect provides instant access to a supportive online community of people in recovery.

Our online recovery meetings are a safe space. They’re open to anyone dealing with substance use, mental health concerns, disordered eating, and any other quality of life concerns.

Meetings are open to all pathways and recovery statuses. They’re led by our certified Peer Support Specialists, compassionate folks who have firsthand experience with recovery.
