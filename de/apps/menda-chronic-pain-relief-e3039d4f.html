Menda provides users with techniques to help alleviate symptoms for many conditions. The app focuses on providing psychoeducation. It is completely free to download and to use.

Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

You've tried everything but the pain continues. Modern neurobiology give hope for reversing chronic pain.

We know this, because we've been there. Us three founders recovered from back pain, IBS, migraines, shoulder pain, and fatigue. How? Using modern nervous system science; we each got to the underlying cause.

1) Follow science-based neural training techniques

2) Work with a 1:1 pain coach to understand your triggers, track symptoms, and keep a log of your progress

3) Return to activities you love

Our participants see dramatic results!

"The app is intuitive and well organized. It explains the latest science on chronic pain in an easy-to-understand way and provides practices that are well designed to manage your pain. I used the app daily -it was like having a coach right by my side, guiding me along the way. Together w/the program, I was able to wash dishes and swim without pain for the first time after six years of chronic pain."

- Tammy A

Content in the app is based on the Novel Psychological Attribution (NPA) and Emotional Awareness and Expression (EAET) protocol. Studies show remarkable improvements over traditional methods.
