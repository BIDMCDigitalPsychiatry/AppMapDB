Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Certified by The American Institute of Stress, PTSD Keys is created for veterans, soldiers, and anyone suffering from Post Traumatic Stress. A 3-tier solution designed to help users manage PTSD symptoms. The app offers one-touch instant relief, a customized reminder that helps predict PTSD reactions, a tab to manage your meditation practice, and a tool to help process the traumatic memories that fuel PTSD. Through brainwave entrainment, the app quickly shuts down the fight-or-flight reaction and activates the body’s natural relaxation response. The app includes a complimentary Stress Is Gone Membership, which provides ongoing aftercare through online tools and can connect you with a live coach if needed.

KEY FEATURES:

Stopper Tab

• One-Touch Instant Relief to manage random stress reactions.

• Predicts and stops routine reactions just before your stress is triggered.

MyMeds Tab

• Structures and maintains your meditation practice.

Resolve Tab

• Revolutionary Tool to Process Traumatic Memories that fuel PTSD reactions.

Also Includes

----------------------

• Complimentary Stress Is Gone Membership; 24/7 access to online relaxation tools (PC and Mobile).

• Stress Stopper Breathwork; 3-step process helps users take control by activating the Relaxation Response whenever they become stressed.

• Coaching; allows you to contact a coach if/when your stress becomes unmanageable.
