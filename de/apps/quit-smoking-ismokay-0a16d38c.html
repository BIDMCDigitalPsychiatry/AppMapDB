Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Quitting smoking is a challenge, but entirely achievable with determination and commitment. Just like thousands of people do it daily, you can also follow this path to success. We are here to help you quit smoking, without pressure, and respecting your own pace.

We have the mission to help you quit smoking and be by your side whenever you need it. Our team is ready to assist you and represents the convergence of experts in various fields:

• Cardiology

• Psychology

• Nutrition

• Spirituality

• Yoga

• Physical Education

Our approach is based on science and facts, and although we may sometimes sound too direct, it's because we are committed to raising awareness about the negative effects of smoking. But don't worry, here, messages of hope, reflection, and motivation prevail.

Discover our benefits.

• CHALLENGES

Embark on a series of challenges that span over 7 days, addressing meditation and prayer, mental training, balanced nutrition, self-awareness of your habits, and a variety of physical activities aimed at strengthening your body and mind.

• BEHAVIORAL CHANGES

The essence of change lies in replacing harmful habits with healthy practices. We provide a space for you to choose which new habits you want to incorporate into your routine.

• MARK THE DATE YOU WILL QUIT SMOKING

Commit to your freedom by marking the day you will definitively leave tobacco behind. In the days leading up to this milestone of your freedom, we will provide you with content that will empower you…
