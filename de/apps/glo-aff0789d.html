Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Embark on an exciting series of journeys as you and your custom GLO pet dive into a new world of information, videos, quizzes and interactive experiences. Along the way, earn stylish rewards to accessorize your GLO pet. By the end of your journey, you’ll have made a new friend and gained new skills to help you manage your mental health and understand safe practices in digital spaces. GLO is presented by It Gets Better Canada (a national registered charity) and made possible through funding from the Department of Women and Gender Equality Canada.
