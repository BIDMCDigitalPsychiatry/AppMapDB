Rezension des Bewerters

Diese Rezension gibt die Meinung der bewertenden Person wieder und stellt keine Empfehlung durch MIND dar.

Stop Drinking Alcohol -Quit! is an app that allows users to track sober days and gives an approximate amount of money that has been saved from being sober. There are also "distractions" features including images, sounds, games, and workouts to allow users to view, listen, and participate instead of drinking.

Note: Privacy policy of this app cannot be accessed from the App Store page.
