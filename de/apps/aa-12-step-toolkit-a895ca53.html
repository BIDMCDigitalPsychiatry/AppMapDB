12 Step Toolkit – Everything AA Members Need for Daily Recovery

12 Step Toolkit is a complete set of recovery tools designed to support your journey—day by day, step by step. Built around the principles found in the Big Book of Alcoholics Anonymous, this app combines guidance, inventories, readings, and practical utilities to help you stay connected to your programme.

Whether you are new to recovery or continuing your journey, 12 Step Toolkit keeps everything you need in one secure, easy-to-use place.

KEY FEATURES

IN-APP SPONSORSHIP

• Connect with thousands of sponsors and sponsees worldwide

• Find a sponsor

• Add sponsees

• Secure, instant in-app messaging

BIG BOOK & STEP WORK

• Full 164 pages of the Big Book of Alcoholics Anonymous

• 70+ additional stories from early editions

• Step-by-step guides on how to work all 12 Steps

• Dedicated sections for completing your own step work

RECOVERY CALCULATOR

• Track your clean or sober time with detailed statistics, averages, and milestones

INVENTORIES & DAILY PRACTICES

• Comprehensive Step 4 moral inventory

• Step 10 spot-check tool

• Interactive “On Awakening” inventory

• Nightly review with reminders

• Hourly mindfulness and consciousness reminders

• The 12 Traditions

• Promises (pp. 83–84)

• Just For Today

• On Awakening
