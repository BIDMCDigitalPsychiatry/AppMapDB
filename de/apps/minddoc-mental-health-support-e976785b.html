Diese Rezension gibt die Meinung der bewertenden Person wieder und stellt keine Empfehlung durch MIND dar.

MindDoc allows users to reflect on potential issues in their life, such as self-image and attitudes towards interpersonal relationships. The app requires users to fill out questionnaires for 14 days in order to view their results. MindDoc partners with BetterHelp, providing users with a link to purchase coaching.
