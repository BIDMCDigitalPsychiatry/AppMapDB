Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Silk + Sonder helps you form happy, healthy habits and brings more positivity into your life every day through a science-backed, community-driven approach. Join tens of thousands in improving their mental health, lowering anxiety and stress, and increasing confidence with guided journaling, active meditation, CBT programming, and self-care games.

Recommended by psychologists, therapists, and mental health experts and loved by members for its one-of-a-kind uplifting community, Silk + Sonder integrates positive psychology and cognitive behavioral therapy so users can easily reflect, connect, and support one another in their self-care journeys.

Silk + Sonder’s app includes:

Daily Affirmations - receive a new affirmation every day to build a positive mindset

Daily Rituals - guided meditations structured as mini podcasts with journaling prompts led by Silk + Sonder’s founder

Sonder Club - private community that promotes a safe, positive, and authentic space for inspiration, accountability, and peer-to-peer support

Daily Questions / Polls - interact with your Sonder Fam community

Self-Care Bingo - recommended activities curated by Silk + Sonder on various topics to encourage healthy habits, creativity, and play offline

Private Memories - house the content of your journals in one place to keep track of your growth and progress over time

Member Discovery - cultivate deeper connections with members who share similar interests, live nearby, or are on a similar journey
