BetterMe: Mental Health—an all-in-one app for your mental well-being, offering meditations, courses, and other tools.

Mindfulness is key to a balanced life, and BetterMe offers simple, practical relaxation methods for everyone, whether you're just starting out or already a pro. Dive into guided practices and start discovering your inner peace today—let's embrace the good vibes together!

Teaming up with mental health experts, we have used effective tools and strategies from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) to help you tune in to the world surrounding you.

Incorporate mindfulness into your daily routine through guided meditations, stress-relief practices, breathwork, sleep meditations, and hundreds of mental health exercises. When your mind is clear, you concentrate better, feel inspired, and get more life energy.

Just a few minutes can really boost your well-being. Opt for quick breathing practices to set the intention for the day or longer meditations to soothe your nerves.

Start your journey with BetterMe as you reduce stress, discover self-love, and enhance your mental well-being.

• Step-by-step plan

Get your daily tasks for morning, afternoon, and evening. Each one consists of a breathing exercise or guided meditation and takes no more than 5 minutes.
