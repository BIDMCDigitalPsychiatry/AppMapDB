Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

Looking for a better way to track your health and treatment journey? Wave Health App is an all-in-one health and symptom tracker for people facing cancer and chronic illness.

Easily keep track of symptoms, medications, and daily activities — and get personal insights to discover what impacts your wellbeing.

Track anything related to your condition all in one place: sleep, water, moods, physical activity, mental health, mindfulness, journal entries, and more. Record important treatment information like medications, symptoms, appointments, and hospital contacts.

As you track, get personalized insights that identify how your actions and activities are related to how you feel. These AI insights take the guesswork out of navigating your condition.

Get answers for common questions like: “Are my medications causing side effects?” and “What activities help boost my mood?” With Wave Health’s insights, finally get the information you need to make a real difference in how you feel and take back control of your health.

Wave Health makes it simple for you to look back on your treatment journey, share detailed information with your care team, and keep your caregivers and loved ones up-to-date. Along the way, learn actionable ways to manage your symptoms, treatments, and overall health.
