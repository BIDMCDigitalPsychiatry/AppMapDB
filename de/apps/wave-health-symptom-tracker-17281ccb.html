Your treatment journey is complex. Wave Health makes it easier.

Track symptoms, management treatments, and take a more active role in your health – all in one place.

Wave Health empowers you to be proactive in reporting symptoms and staying connected to their care team between appointments. By identifying trends early, Wave Health uncovers patterns in your health, helping you better understand and manage your well-being.

Log how you’re feeling, record ongoing symptoms, manage medications with automated alerts, and monitor important daily activities like sleep, water, steps and vitals. Through personalized guidance, educational resources, and tailored insights, Wave Health helps you navigate treatment and walk into appointments with a record instead of a recollection.

Two ways to use Wave Health

Wave Health works as an independent app, or connected to your hospital and care team.

Key App Features:

Symptom Logging: Track your daily symptoms and keep a log to bring to your care team – sleep, water, moods, physical activity, mental health, mindfulness, journal entries, and more.

Medication Tracking: Stay on schedule with medication reminders to never miss a dose.

Custom Trackers & Quick Logs: Capture symptoms, vitals, body functions, and activities unique to your journey, not just what's on a standard list.

Tailored Insights: Your logged data becomes actionable insights showing how your daily habits affect your wellbeing.
