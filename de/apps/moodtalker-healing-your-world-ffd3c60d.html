MoodTalker - Your AI compassionate friend that will help you boost your mental health 24/7.

Feeling down and need a boost? Look no further than the MoodTalker. We offer a private space to share your worries without judgment. MoodTalker - An AI companion for your everyday emotional needs.

Whether you need a compassionate friend or an AI to talk to, the MoodTalker - MoodTalker is your right choice. Install the app for free, share your thoughts, write whatever is on your mind, and take some mind reading cards to lift your mood instantly.

Key Features:

##Empathetic Conversations with Adorable Friends

Need a compassionate friend during your lowest points? Meet Finnick the witty fox, Daisy the gentle deer, and Bruno the warm bear. These AI friends understand your emotions, play soothing music, and guide you through deep breaths. After each chat, they'll leave you a comforting journal entry to write your thoughts.

##Share Your Thoughts & Emotions

MoodTalker allows you to safely share your thoughts and feelings through text or voice. This mood tracker measures your emotional state and uses psychological techniques to help organize your mind. You'll receive personalized advice based on your emotional state and can track your emotional journey with insightful mood graphs.
