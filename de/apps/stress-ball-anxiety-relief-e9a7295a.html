Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

It's not a great feeling to be anxious. And when you are, it's tough to meditate without having the stressful thoughts slip back in. Fortunately, with Stress Ball, you can interact with your phone and clear your mind with exercises, fidgets and videos within the app. There are five different tabs you can use to calm down: Breathe, Fidget, ChillTok, Color & Reflect

In the Breathe tab, there are breathing exercises to help you take deep breaths and calm down. You can customize your experience with relaxing sounds and backgrounds. The phone also vibrates and provides various animations to distract your thoughts.

In the Fidget tab, there are virtual fidgets and toys you can play with to distract your mind and let out all of your anxious energy on the app. The fidgets also provide vibration and audio feedback to help you feel more relaxed.

In the Feel tab, you can tap on ASMR sounds and feel the vibrations of the sounds!! These vibrations are super calming to the body and mind. Give it a try!!

In the Color tab, you can complete vibrating color by numbers. Coloring can help soothe your mind and distract you away from your stress. It also is great for fidgeting when feeling anxious or restless.
