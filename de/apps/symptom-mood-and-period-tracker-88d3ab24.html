Feeling overwhelmed by your health? Take control of your symptoms, flare-ups, and overall well-being.

CareClinic turns your symptoms, medications, and habits into personalized insights and daily actions. Built on proven clinical frameworks to help you make measurable progress every day.

Join over 500,000 people managing their Chronic Illnesses, Migraine, Pain, Headaches, Fatigue, IBS, POTS, PCOS, EDS, Period, Mood, Mental Health, and more!

Build healthy habits, stay accountable, and act on insights

- Import or add your personal health records

- Enable trackers for meds, symptoms, measurements, activity, nutrition, and more

- Add medication reminders, symptom check-up reminders, and appointment reminders

- Invite your caregivers and dependents, such as kids or parents

- Connect your wearables data and your add health goals

CareClinic becomes your companion as you put it all into action. Within days, you will start uncovering triggers, spotting correlations, and seeing how your Pain, Headaches, Migraines, Periods, IBS, PCOS, and Mood are affected, helping you take back control of your health and share the right information with your doctor for more personalized treatments.

Trackers included:

- Medication Tracker for prescriptions, birth control, vitamins, supplements

- Symptoms Tracker acts as your symptoms diary with visual body mapping for keeping things like a pain log, flare-ups, and more

- Measurements tracker to track vitals such as blood pressure, heart rate,…
