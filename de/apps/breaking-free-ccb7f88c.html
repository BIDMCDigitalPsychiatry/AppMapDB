Beschreibung aus dem App-Store – nicht von MIND geprüft

PLEASE NOTE: To set up an account on Breaking Free, you will need an Access Code from a service that is supporting you.

Breaking Free is a confidential support program designed to help you manage your relationship with alcohol and other substances. It gives you powerful strategies you can access 24/7 and at your own pace.

Why use Breaking Free?

• Tailored support – Tell us what specific challenges you are facing and get tailored support to help you achieve your goals.

• Personalised diagram – Get a diagram that is unique to you and shows you the best pathway to achieve lasting change.

• Effective strategies – Learn coping skills to help you build healthier habits.

• Action Plans – When you complete each strategy, get a summary to guide you on how to put your new skill into practice.

• Real-time alerts – Receive location alerts to help you manage your challenging situations, and calendar alerts to nudge you to carry out your planned activities.

• Progress tracking – Stay motivated by earning achievements and seeing all your gains on your own personal dashboard.

• Confidential and secure – Your data will be completely private and protected at all times.

Disclaimer: This app is a digital program designed to help people change their behaviours. It does not provide any health or medical information, diagnoses, or treatment advice.
