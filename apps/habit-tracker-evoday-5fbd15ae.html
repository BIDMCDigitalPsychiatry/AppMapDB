Visual habit tracker to build good habits, break bad ones, and stay motivated with grids, calendars & widgets. Used by hundreds of thousands!

▶ HOW IT WORKS

• Create the habits you want to build or break

• Log your progress every day

• See your streaks, completion rates, and long-term progress

• Stay motivated with beautiful calendars, grids, and widgets

▶ WHY IT WORKS

• Big changes come from small actions repeated over time

• Clear visual progress helps you stay consistent

• Streaks, stats, and widgets keep your habits top of mind

Evoday helps you stay motivated and keep going, day after day.

▶ FEATURES

• Build good habits and break bad ones

• Track your progress on beautiful grids and calendars

• See your current streak, longest streak, and completion rates

• Home Screen widgets: weekly calendar, monthly calendar, last 3 months, last 20 weeks, and more

• Share beautifully formatted habit calendars

• Export your data and keep your own backup

• Your habit data never leaves your phone, except in your iCloud backups
