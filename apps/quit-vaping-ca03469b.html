Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Quit Vaping provides users with community support and uses summaries of data to motivate them to continue quitting vaping. When paying for a yearly or monthly subscription, users are able to access more features such as coaching support and a personalized "quit plan tracker".
