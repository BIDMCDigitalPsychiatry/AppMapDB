Innerworld is a mental health app that uses Cognitive Behavioral Immersion (CBI) to provide users with support for issues such as anxiety, depression, ADHD, etc.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Innerworld is an award-winning mental health program that has helped more than 100,000 people. You'll get life-changing tools to help with your toughest challenges, alongside a supportive community of people who understand what you're going through. Attend any of the more than 100 support groups led by trained Guides on stress, anxiety, depression, ADHD, and more.

You’ll learn proven, science-based skills in an immersive environment — we call this Cognitive Behavioral Immersion™ (CBI). These tools will help you manage daily anxiety, relieve stress, combat depression, address loneliness, improve your overall mental health, and more. Innerworld delivers results similar to therapy — at a fraction of the cost.

About Innerworld:

BE WITH PEOPLE WHO GET YOU

At the heart of Innerworld is community. People from all over the globe connecting, healing and growing. Together.

REMAIN ANONYMOUS

Create an avatar and share your story without having to share your face.

ATTEND UNLIMITED MENTAL HEALTH EVENTS

Join any of 100+ live anonymous group events each week, all led by trained Guides. Event topics include stress, worry, general anxiety, health anxiety, depression, relationships, parenting, grief, loss, ADHD, trauma, addiction, mindfulness, and more. You can also attend meditations, social events, or get creative in the art gallery. There’s no limit to the number of events you can attend.
