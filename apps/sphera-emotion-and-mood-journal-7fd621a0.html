How apps are evaluated

Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

The app is listed under different names across stores: “Sphera: Emotion & Mood Tracker” / “Journaling & Emotion Tracker” on the Apple App Store and “Sphera: Emotion & Mood Journal” on Google Play. Developer is 4A Labs, Inc. The app is free to download; premium features require a subscription (1 month $9.99, 6 months $44.99, 12 months $34.99 USD on iOS). No lifetime or one-time purchase option was found. [Features behind the paywall: ___.]

The privacy policy (effective 2023-11-06) states that personal information (email, first and last name) is retained by the developer on its servers and that third-party service providers, including Google Play Services and Facebook, have access to personal information. Log data (IP address, device name, OS version, usage time) is collected through third-party products. The policy does not provide a data deletion or data access right for adult users and does not name specific security measures such as encryption.

No peer-reviewed studies of this app were found. No crisis hotline or emergency guidance was found in the store listing [or in the app]. The store listing does not state that the app is not a replacement for professional care. From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

While you can’t always control what happens in life, you have the power to control how you respond. By learning to navigate emotions and tackle challenges in healthier ways, you can regain control and create a better life.

Sphera is your personal mental health companion, designed to help you understand and manage your emotions with ease. Whether you're looking to boost your mood, build emotional resilience, or practice mindfulness, Sphera offers guided self-reflection, emotion tracking, and stress-relief techniques tailored to your needs.

Key Features:

- Emotion Tracking: Monitor your emotional patterns over time and gain insights into your feelings.

- Daily Prompts: Thoughtful questions to guide your self-reflection, boost self-awareness, and promote personal growth.

- Mindfulness Practices: Simple exercises to reduce stress, enhance emotional clarity, and improve mental well-being.

- Set Well Being Goals: track your daily wellbeing habits.

- Gratitude Journal: Cultivate a positive mindset with daily reflections.

- Stress-Relief Techniques: Quick, effective methods to help you relax and regain balance.

- Emotional Intelligence: Exercises designed to improve your understanding of emotions and how to respond effectively.

Sphera creates a supportive space for self-discovery, empowering you to enhance emotional intelligence, build resilience, and take control of your emotional health.
