MEDICAL DISCLAIMER

OCD Budz is a self-help tool that complements professional treatment. It does not replace therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment from qualified healthcare providers. Always consult a licensed mental health professional before making treatment decisions.

If experiencing a mental health crisis, contact emergency services or call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

Evidence-Based Support for OCD Management

OCD Budz helps individuals manage Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder using Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) - the gold standard treatment recommended by mental health professionals worldwide.

CORE THERAPEUTIC FEATURES

ERP Practice Tools Practice systematic exposure with guided timers that help you delay compulsive responses. Build tolerance gradually and document your experiences.

Grounding Exercises Access immediate support during anxiety with evidence-based breathing and grounding techniques.

Audio Exposure Library Record personalized exposure scripts addressing your specific obsessions. Practice systematic desensitization at your own pace.

Environmental Trigger Mapping Document locations that trigger obsessive thoughts. Receive mindful notifications to help you prepare mentally and practice coping strategies in real-world settings.

Progress Documentation Maintain a therapeutic journal to track thoughts, emotional states, and behavioral patterns. Monitor how your symptoms change over time.
