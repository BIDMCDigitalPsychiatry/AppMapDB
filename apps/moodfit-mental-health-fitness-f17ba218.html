How Moodfit Helps You:

Moodfit offers a complete set of mental health tools in one app:

• Mood journal – track emotions and discover patterns

• CBT tools – challenge and change unhelpful thoughts

• Mindfulness & meditation – reduce stress and increase presence

• Breathwork – calm anxiety and regulate your nervous system

• Gratitude journal – train your brain to notice positives

• Nervous system - see how your nervous system is affecting your emotions

• Daily goals – build mental fitness habits that stick

• Insights – see how sleep, exercise, nutrition, and social interactions affect mood

• Medication & custom activity tracking – learn what works best for you

• Validated assessments – monitor depression (PHQ-9) and anxiety (GAD-7) over time

• Education & inspiration – practical content on rumination, procrastination,…
