From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Discover the toxic beliefs that may be holding you back.

Understand why you feel and behave the way you do.

Take daily steps toward feeling calmer, more resilient and self-aware.

Thinkladder is a mental wellbeing app that enables you to explore your belief system, weed out and replace limiting ideas that stop you from living your best life.

Thinkladder gives you simple CBT and ACT-based tools and methods, proven to improve your mental wellbeing. It only takes a few minutes each day to understand yourself better and move towards the change you want.

With Thinkladder you can:

- Explore themes like Perfectionism, Self Worth, Social Distancing, Anxiety, Body Image, and Anger, and discover underlying limiting beliefs.

- Challenge limiting beliefs with insights collated by mental health professionals.

- Set time and location reminders for your selected insights.

- Use journal prompts written by therapists.

- Listen to audio practices that guide you through therapeutic visualisation and breathing techniques.
