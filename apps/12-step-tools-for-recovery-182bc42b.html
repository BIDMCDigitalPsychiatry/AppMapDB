Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

AA Big Book is an app geared towards Christians who are quitting an addiction to alcohol. The app features a journal, community support message boards, aa tool to find AA meetings, audio information clips from the Alcoholics Anonymous Big Book and prayers.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Carry essential resources and an active, supportive community wherever you go!

Join the most active sober community on any app, and have every resource you could need in one place. Over 100,000+ individuals trust it to provide them with the support they need on their journey, one day at a time.

Unlock the complete text and audio of foundational material, reflections for each day, resources to help you stay sober, and much more!

Foundational Material

- Immerse yourself in the content spread across 164 pages, including prayers, personal stories, and insights into the steps and traditions.

- Includes the full Big Book text, 12 Steps and 12 Traditions (12 and 12), As Bill Sees it, Daily Reflections, Prayers, Living Sober, the Joe and Charlie Audio Tapes, and much more!

- Easily navigate through pages and chapters with search and highlight features, ensuring a smooth reading experience.

- Customize your reading with adjustable font sizes and Light and Dark Mode options for comfortable reading anytime, anywhere.

- Have the cornerstone material always at hand, offering constant guidance and support in your journey towards sobriety and personal development.

Podcasts and Audiobooks

- Explore a variety of podcasts and audiobooks from individuals sharing their journey to recovery.

- Discover new content contributed by community members or share your own story with others.
