How apps are evaluated

Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

App that allows users to progress towards smoking cessation with the help of medical providers. However, the medical providers associated to the app reside in India and users need to pay for cessation courses to fully use the app. While the app does offer virtual support it is intended to be used in conjunction with in-person centers found primarily in India.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Innovative Cancer Care Tobacco Wellness App provides a tobacco user step by step guide for de-addiction aided by a counselor or a doctor. Users can assess Will Power Index, Addiction Level and Type of Dependence with ease. Based on the assessed level, the appropriate treatment process can be chosen for treatment. An interactive and engaging application which keeps the tobacco addict engaged during his/her quit journey.

The application provides unique capabilities to counselors and doctors for maintaining health screening records of patients. The application offers medical professionals with a systematic process integrating behavioural counselling with existent healthcare procedures.

Salient Features of the Application are:

• Maintain Health Screening Records: General parameters like blood pressure, glucose, etc. anthropometric measurements like height, weight etc, physical examination of audiometry and optical parameters and others.

• Maintain records for self-assessment tests like 4 Finger test, Will power Index, Addiction Level, Dependency Level, Direct Expenses etc.

• Maintain Treatment Progress Records - Record cravings, withdrawal symptoms, and others

• Maintain history of examinations, counselling and treatment records

• Maintain Prescription records generated on system

• Maintain Counselling Reports generated on system

• Get online counselling for a tobacco user by a counselor or doctor.

• Get medical consultation from a medical expert/specialist.
