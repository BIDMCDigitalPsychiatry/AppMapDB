WHO-validated quit smoking app with 4.5M+ users. Start your smoke-free life today!

Ready to quit smoking for good? Kwit combines proven science with gamification to make quitting easier and engaging. As the first WHO-validated smoking cessation app, Kwit helps you break free from nicotine addiction using evidence-based cognitive behavioral therapy techniques that work.

Track your progress in real-time

Watch your quit smoking journey unfold with personalized statistics showing smoke-free days, money saved, cigarettes avoided, and health improvements. Every milestone becomes powerful motivation to stay tobacco-free. See exactly how your body heals as you progress through your transformation.

Science-backed craving management

Beat cravings with CBT-based tools including guided breathing exercises, mini-games, and instant motivation cards. Turn withdrawal moments into opportunities for growth with techniques developed by addiction specialists. When nicotine cravings hit, you'll have proven strategies to overcome them naturally.

Join the quit smoking community

Connect with supportive groups where fellow quitters share victories, challenges, and daily encouragement. Exchange tips with people who understand your journey. You're never alone in your mission to become smoke-free. Get real-time support from thousands of successful ex-smokers worldwide.
