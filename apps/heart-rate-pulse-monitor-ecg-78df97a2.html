Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Monitor your vital signs with Heart Rate — a precise tool for tracking your pulse, blood pressure, and oxygen levels.

Key Health Tracking Features:

• Heartbeat Tracker & Pulse Monitor: Easily log your heart rate and track your pulse history with intuitive charts.

• Blood Oxygen Monitoring: Keep a detailed record of your blood oxygen and oxygen saturation (SpO2) levels.

• Blood Pressure Log: Track your blood pressure readings to monitor your cardiovascular trends.

• Heartbeat Monitor & Pulse: Stay updated on your heart rhythm and pulse patterns through easy data entry.

• Temperature & Fever Check: Log your body temperature to monitor fever and wellness.

• Symptom Tracker: Use the integrated health journal to record symptoms and general wellbeing.

Smart Data Analysis:

Gain personalized insights by analyzing your health history alongside your age and gender. All your data is visualized in easy-to-read graphs.

Professional PDF Export:

Export your pulse, blood pressure, and symptom logs into a PDF report for medical consultations.

PLEASE PAY ATTENTION:

While our application can assist in recording heart rate data, it relies on user input and integration with other devices.

Also, you cannot directly measure blood pressure and temperature in the application - they are calculated based on heart rate and other entered personal data. The accuracy of the data depends on the accuracy of the information you provide.
