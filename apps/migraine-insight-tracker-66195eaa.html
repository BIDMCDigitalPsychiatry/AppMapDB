Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

This app helps users track their migraines and different symptoms. The app has automatic tracking of weather, steps, sleep, location and workout. It uses various symptoms to find patterns and identify migraine triggers. The app is free to download but requires a subscription to unlock premium features.

PINPOINT YOUR REAL TRIGGERS

Finding triggers helps people reduce or eliminate headaches. Track symptoms, medications, triggers, and more. We've helped thousands of people get better by finally finding their real triggers.

CHRONIC MIGRAINE 'PAIN LEVEL TRACKING' MODE

The app works great for all headache types. And, for people living with chronic migraine, there's a pain level mode. This helps you record pain levels instead of episodes.

We've worked hard to make this tracker work great for people who live with all types of headache disorders.

HOW IT WORKS

You track information about your symptoms and suspected triggers. Migraine Insight combines this with data you're already collecting in other apps (e.g. sleep, weather, workouts). Then, powerful analysis helps you make better insights from your tracking.
