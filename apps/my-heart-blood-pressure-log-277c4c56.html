From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

My Heart is the blood pressure log trusted by millions of people worldwide. Record your readings, understand your trends, and share clear reports with your doctor — all in one app.

Your blood pressure data stays on your device. No account required. No data sold or shared with third parties.

This app is not a medical device. It does not measure blood pressure or provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider before making health-related decisions.

Breathe Before You Measure

A guided breathing exercise helps you relax before taking a blood pressure reading. Five cycles of slow breathing — about one minute. Stress and anxiety can raise your reading by 10–20 points, so a calmer measurement is a more accurate one. No other app on Android includes this.

Updated for 2025 AHA/ACC Guidelines

Classify your blood pressure readings using the latest 2025 AHA/ACC (American), ESH/ESC (European), or JNC7 standards. Choose the guideline your doctor follows. Categories are color-coded from normal through the stages of hypertension, so you always know where you stand.

Simple Logging with Context

Record systolic, diastolic, pulse, weight, and blood oxygen level in seconds. Add tags like «morning», «after medication», or «after exercise» to capture what affects your readings. Every reading is saved with a timestamp and color-coded classification.
