Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

CALMzone is our free app designed to help you deal with anxiety and look after your head.

CALMzone gives you practical tools to improve your mood and find stress relief - all from your pocket. It’s full of exercises and info to help you manage the good stuff, the tough stuff, and everything in between.

**Key features - at a glance**

- Guided tools to calm anxiety and cope with overthinking

- Helpful meditation app features for daily practice

- Daily anxiety relief to reduce stress

- Panic attack relief

- Space to understand and process negative thoughts

- Therapeutic activities created with mental health experts

- Gentle support for clearing mental fog and grounding your emotions

**ORCHA Assured Bronze Accreditation**

“Looking after your head can be overwhelming. That’s where our app comes in. It tailors support to your needs and makes it easier for you to find the techniques that work for you. Mental wellbeing isn’t one-size-fits-all - CALMzone helps you find what works.” - Wendy Robinson, Director of Services at CALM
