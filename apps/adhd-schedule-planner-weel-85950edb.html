ADHD Visual Planner is an app intended to help people with dyscalculia and time blindness organize their calendar and stay on top of their events. It is free to download but includes monthly and yearly subscriptions to unlock additional features.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Time is a slippery thing—but with the Weel ADHD planner, it's all under control. Weel transforms your daily schedule into a clear, single image, using an intuitive 24-hour analog watchface that provides a full-circle view of your time. This unique feature is especially helpful for people with ADHD, dyscalculia, or time blindness. Even if you're neurotypical, the Weel ADHD planner offers a refreshing, visual way to manage time effectively!

Whether you’re juggling tasks, working across time zones, or avoiding last-minute scrambles, Weel’s design helps everyone stay organized and on track.

Key Features of Weel ADHD Planner:

• Stay on top of your schedule: The 24-hour analog watchface allows you to see your entire day at a glance, helping you focus on upcoming tasks and avoid losing track of time before important events.

• Plan ahead: Attach notes or checklists to events so you remember exactly what to prepare for meetings or appointments.

• Factor in travel time: The Weel ADHD planner helps you account for travel between events, so you avoid scheduling conflicts and always arrive on time.

• Manage time zones easily: Simplify scheduling for international meetings or travel plans without the confusion.

• Customizable for your needs: Weel is highly configurable, allowing you to tailor the app to your own preferences and organize your day your way.
