How apps are evaluated

Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Borderline Personality Tracker provides users with a symptom tracker that allows them to make daily entries, using a scale of "awful, bad, ok, good, awesome" for each symptom. The tracker also allows them to input the treatments that they have used that day (such as medication or talk therapy) and has a "notes" section for journalling. Users can generate a 7-day (free version), 30-day, 90-day or 1 year (paid version) summary chart report which can be shared through social media or sent to a mental health professional.
