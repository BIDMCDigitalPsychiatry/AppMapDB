Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Quit Anything- X days since is an app where users can list bad habits and associated money and time wasted on those habits. These inputs are shown as a day counter view. It requires $2.99 upgrade to list multiple bad habits and export data. With the free version, only a single habit can be monitored and data cannot be exported.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Quit Anything helps you get rid of bad habits in a very easy way. All you need to do is:

- List your bad habits

- Enter how much money and time you waste on them

- View the day counter increase as every day passes

- Calculate your total saved time and money

- Add custom fields
