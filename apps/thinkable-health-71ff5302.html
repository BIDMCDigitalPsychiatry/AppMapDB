Thinkable Health: Your Companion for Coping with Medical and Mental Conditions

Can better thinking patterns help you cope with chronic pain, migraines, tinnitus, and other medical conditions?

The answer is YES!

Research shows Thinkable helps users improve their mental health and coping skills by training daily for just 14 days. Created by Dr. Guy Doron, clinical therapist and mobile health expert, Thinkable is backed by research and designed to enhance your thought processes, boost confidence, and improve mood—all without typing a single line.

Thinkable is a smart, personalized tool to help you face the challenges of chronic conditions, improve your mental well-being, and foster personal growth.

HOW IT WORKS

- Learn to embrace positive thinking and develop resilience in the face of chronic symptoms

- Track your mood and pain levels to see how your inner dialogue transforms

- View a visual journal of your progress and symptom management

- Train daily for 14 days to make self-talk your most powerful coping tool

IS IT LIKE THERAPY?

Thinkable incorporates key elements of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), transforming them into an accessible, engaging app. While not a replacement for person-to-person therapy, it empowers you to practice self-care by engaging with your thoughts and developing healthy thinking patterns—all while tracking your mood and symptoms.
