Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Sober shouldn’t mean somber! Meet Loosid, the ultimate sobriety companion app for anyone looking to join a robust sober and alcohol-free community!

Loosid is a free sober app for iOS that features a sobriety tracker, daily tips, sober community, gratitude practice, dating, and more. Download now to stay connected.

With Loosid, you'll have access to a wealth of features designed to support your sobriety journey. With features such as daily inspiration to expanding your sober grid with events and meetups, Loosid has everything you need to stay on track, one day at a time.

At the heart of the Loosid app is its thriving community component. The app allows users to create profiles, post updates, and interact with other members of the social community through a variety of chats and posts.

In addition to its community feature, the Loosid app also provides users with access to a range of resources that can help them stay sober. Loosid is the guide to living sober and alcohol-free: whether you want to set a clean time counter, connect with other sober members, listen to audio episodes of how people have overcome alcohol or addiction, or date other clean singles, Loosid is for you!

One of the unique aspects of the Loosid community is its diversity. The app is not limited to people who are in recovery from alcohol or drug addiction, but also caters to those who have chosen to live a sober lifestyle for personal or health reasons.
