App that allows you to set reminders.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Most reminder apps tell you what to do.

Sup helps you remember why.

This isn’t about streaks or productivity hacks. Sup sends reminders written in your own voice. Simple nudges that show up at the right time, not the same time.

Some are small: “Drink a glass of water.”

Some are real: “Call Mom.”

Some are deep: “Remember how that night went when you got too drunk.”

Every message feels personal and alive. They’re unpredictable, human, and designed to make you pause for a second and act with intention.

Why Sup works

* Random timing keeps your brain from tuning out

* Natural language makes reminders feel real

* Simple templates help you write prompts that matter

Use Sup to:

* Check in with yourself

* Stay connected to the people you care about

* Build better routines without the pressure

* Reflect, reset, and keep moving forward

Sup isn’t another habit tracker.

It’s a nudge system for your real life.

Do what matters, when it matters, in a voice that sounds like you.
