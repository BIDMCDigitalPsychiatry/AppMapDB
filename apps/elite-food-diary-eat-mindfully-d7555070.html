Introducing Elite, Your Simple and Beautiful Food Diary

Welcome to Elite, the simplest and most visually stunning food diary app you'll ever need. With its fluid and captivating layout, Elite keeps you at the forefront of your daily meals and effortlessly syncs data from HealthKit, including steps, sleep, and calorie burn.

Stay Present, Focus on Today:

Elite empowers you to focus on the present moment. Embrace each day as a new journey towards a better you, and let Elite be your trusted companion to track your entire day's meals in one convenient place.

Seamless Glances at Your Progress:

Get a comprehensive overview of your day on Elite Home View, where your meals and valuable HealthKit data blend seamlessly. Witness your progress in a glance, all presented with elegance and simplicity.

Delve into Your History:

With Elite's History View, you can effortlessly explore your past meals and activities. Its functional layout offers both power and beauty, providing you with a complete perspective on your wellness journey.

Collect Points, Unlock Achievements:

Elite introduces a unique points system that adds an element of gamification to your food tracking experience. Collect points as you make mindful choices and unlock achievements that celebrate your progress.
