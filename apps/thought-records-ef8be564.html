Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Thought Records does exactly what the name promises. It's a clean notes app built around thought recording in a CBT style; you log a situation, write down the positive and negative thoughts associated , and then the app circles back to ask whether your perspective has changed, which is nice because it encourages reflection as opposed to just venting into a notes app. It is not trying to be a full mental health suite which I appreciate; the entry is quick and the prompts are very simple; "add a positive/negative thought" and "how do you feel about this situation?". Would like to see features like how perspective has changed over time or tagging recurring situations. It's a no-nonsense app for working through thoughts.
