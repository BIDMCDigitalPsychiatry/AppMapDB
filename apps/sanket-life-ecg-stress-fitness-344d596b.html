Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Understanding your heart’s activity is an important part of maintaining overall wellness. The Sanket App, when paired with the SanketLife Device, empowers users to record and observe their heart’s electrical patterns as part of their personal wellness journey.

Designed for ease of use and portability, the SanketLife Device allows users to manually capture ECG recordings in response to occasional symptoms such as mild discomfort.

The Sanket system is not intended for continuous monitoring or real-time diagnostics but can support individuals in improving their health awareness and maintaining proactive communication with their care providers.

This wellness solution may support individuals who:

* Are tracking changes in lifestyle or heart wellness over time

* Wish to share episodic ECG recordings with their physician

* Are recovering from a cardiac procedure and maintaining general awareness
