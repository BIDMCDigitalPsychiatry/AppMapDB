What is Petit BamBou?

Petit BamBou is Europe’s No. 1 mindfulness meditation and breathing app. Designed for everyone; from absolute beginners to advanced practitioners. Our mission is to help you introduce wellness, calm, and daily introspection into your life. We provide real, human-centric tools to help you establish sustainable daily routines that actively support your mental health.

What makes Petit BamBou unique compared to other meditation apps?

Unlike other apps, Petit BamBou features 100% human-created content with absolutely no AI-generated writing or voices. Every session is crafted and narrated by local psychologists, psychiatrists, and meditation experts to ensure authentic, empathetic guidance.

Can I use Petit BamBou in my language?

Our sessions are natively recorded in 6 languages: English, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, and German.

What features are included in the app?

Our vast catalog includes over 878 guided meditations and more than 83 specialized programs. We cover every aspect of modern life, including:

Stress & Burnout: Practical tools for workplace anxiety and daily stress regulation.

Life Stages: Dedicated tracks for motherhood, kids (ages 3–12), and senior wellness.

Specialized Health: Guided support for coping with addiction, pain, and sleep issues.

Relaxation Tools: A comprehensive sound library for deep concentration and sleep, featuring advanced binaural audio and relaxing music.
