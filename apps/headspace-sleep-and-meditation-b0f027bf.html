How apps are evaluated

Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Headspace is an app that aids users with mental health topics related to anxiety and stress, sleep, work, etc. This app allows users to sign up for a free trial for 14 days, after which a subscription must be purchased in order to continue using the app. Monthly subscription costs are > $10 USD/CAD, which may be costly for users. A cheaper annual subscription is available, costing under > $10 USD/CAD. Youth may be able to access this application through their organization for free, such as through school boards, see your organizational contacts for more information.
