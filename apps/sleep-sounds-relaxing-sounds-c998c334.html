Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

There are sleep sounds in the app which can be mixed as per users' preference. There is also an option for a sleep timer that turns off the sound after a particular period. There is a subscription for the premium version which is ad-free and has unlimited premium features.
