Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

App allows users to track their daily cigarette use and set goals for reducing the amount smoked. It has reminders to notify when users can smoke again and gradually reduces the amount users can smoke while tracking financial befits of smoking cessation. Free version has some limited adds but paid version is available to remove these.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Quit smoking with Smoking Log by gradually reducing the number of cigarettes you smoke daily. Set goals and track your smoking habits easily. View history to track your smoking habits and progress over time.

History View:

★ Allows easy comparisons of statistics between days

★ Drill down into the summary view for previous days

Chart View:

★ Visualize your progress across time

Miscellaneous:
