Orca is an app that allows you to create and share (sharing optional) gratitude messages and pictures to yourself and others in your network.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Orca (formerly Happyfeed) is a shareable photo and video diary built on the practice of gratitude journaling. Post daily positive moments to improve your mood and focus more on the bright side. Plus, you'll be creating an record of all your best memories. Keep it private or use groups as a mini social network - sharing select memories with friends and family.

• Add multiple photos, video & location

• Widgets: Daily throwbacks and shared memories

• Group sharing for couples, friends & family

• Unique prompts, quotes, and fun facts every day

• Gratitude Jar to view random past posts

• All posts are 100% private by default

• Tag your best days with an emoji

• Unlimited days you can post for free

• Passcode & lock screen with Face ID

• PDF/CSV Export for printing (Plus only)

Explore Your Past Memories - After one week, you can view memories in your gratitude journal from a week ago. This period grows to months and even years. You can also see random moments using the Gratitude Jar. Each month, create a recap video of your favorite photos and clips.

Journaling Together - Pods are private groups for sharing memories and send emoji reactions. Send only selected photos, videos, and text to your friends, partner, or family. It's perfect for long distance relationships and faraway family.
