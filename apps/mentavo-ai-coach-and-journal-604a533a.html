Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

An AI coach that knows your everyday life.

No more explaining yourself from scratch. Mentavo knows your journal, check-ins, goals and routines – and, if you choose, your calendar and sleep data. Say what's on your mind, and Mentavo turns it into one concrete next step. Nothing is created until you confirm.

TALK IT THROUGH

• One coach for everything: sort things out, think through a decision, move forward – no mode to pick.

• Mentavo remembers what matters from your conversations. If something changes, your latest words count.

• Voice mode (Pro): just talk – Mentavo replies in the chat.

WRITE IT DOWN

• Journal: write freely or start with a single prompt. Dictate instead of typing.

• Check-in: log your mood in seconds – add feelings, energy and what helped today if you like.

ACT ON IT

• Goals with doable steps, routines with reminders.

• Reminders: set them yourself or just tell your coach: "Remind me tomorrow at 8 to call the doctor."

• Plan by chat: "Schedule a call with my manager on Friday at 2 pm for 30 minutes." Mentavo prepares the event, you confirm.

• Today: one focus instead of a long list – and the reason why.

SEE THE PATTERNS

• Analysis: mood, journaling, goals and routines over weeks – summarized, not judged.

• Mentavo Map: what tends to show up together in your days, and how many days that is based on.

• Apple Health (optional): sleep, steps and workouts add context. Mentavo only reads and never writes.
