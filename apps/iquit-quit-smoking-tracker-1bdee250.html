Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Quitting smoking happens one choice at a time. iQuit gives you a clear place to handle cravings, see your progress, and keep moving forward—even after a slip.

START WITH A PLAN

Add your quit date, usual cigarettes per day, pack price, and the reason you want to quit. iQuit turns those details into progress you can see.

GET THROUGH THE NEXT CRAVING

Open the 60-second craving reset when an urge hits. Breathe, notice what triggered it, and record the moment when it passes.

SEE WHAT YOU ARE GAINING

Follow your smoke-free time, estimated cigarettes avoided, money saved, and recovery milestones.

A SLIP DOES NOT ERASE YOUR WORK

Record a cigarette without restarting your entire journey. iQuit adjusts your estimates while preserving the progress you already made.

FREE FEATURES

• Personal quit plan

• Live smoke-free timer

• Cigarettes avoided and estimated savings

• 60-second craving support

• Slip tracking

• Recent activity and recovery milestones

IQUIT PRO

An optional subscription unlocks full craving and slip history, trigger insights, daily reminders, and the complete recovery timeline. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

iQuit supports behavior change but does not diagnose, treat, or replace medical care. For treatment or medication advice, speak with a healthcare professional.
