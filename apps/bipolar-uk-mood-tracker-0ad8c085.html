Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Bipolar UK’s Mood Tracker app allows you to record your daily mood, medications, emotions and more! It helps you:

• Track your mood using the Bipolar UK Mood Scale

• Build a clearer picture of your wellbeing over time

• Recognise early changes and spot patterns

• Share helpful insights with your healthcare team

Understanding your mood patterns can help you feel more in control.

People using the app have told us it has helped them better manage their condition, take action sooner and get more out of appointments with healthcare professionals.
