How apps are evaluated

Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Not listed on the App Store as OCD.app-self help + therapy (US,France, Germany) or "OCD.app - Anxiety, PTSD, CBT"(Canada,UK); it now covers OCD, PTSD, anxiety, depression, and related concerns. Free version includes 50+ exercises; full access is 19.99/month or 78.99/year, and a separate "human assistant" plan is 99.99/month. Registration is optional (sign-in available via Apple, Google, or Facebook). Users can share their "thoughts" publicly with other users and to social media. Per the privacy policy, usage analytics are shared with Google Analytics, Facebook ads, and Appsflyer for advertising , and Datta is processed in Isreal, the US, and the EU. Data deletion is available to all users via ggtude.com/data-deletion; access and portability rights are listed only for EU/Uk residents. Peer-reviewed studies of this app exist, including RCTs. List "suicidal thoughts" as a target concern, but no crisis resources were found in the listing.
