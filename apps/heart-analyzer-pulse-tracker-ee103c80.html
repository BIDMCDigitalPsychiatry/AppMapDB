Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Heart Analyzer helps you track Heart Rate, HRV, Resting Heart Rate, Cardio Recovery, Sleep and ECG trends using Apple Watch.

Analyze your Health data with advanced charts, insights, Heart Reports, widgets and detailed heart analytics powered by Apple Health.

Set your own high and low heart rate alerts, take control of your HRV data in the new HRV Center, and see your sleep in detail with multiple sessions, naps and time out of bed.

------------------------------------------------

■ Simple PRIVACY

------------------------------------------------

◎ No third party analytics

◎ No data ever leaves your device

◎ No modifications to your health data

------------------------------------------------

■ FEATURES

------------------------------------------------

◎ The Dashboard - Where you can swipe between days, scroll through summaries and tap into the details

◎ Charts - Heart Rate and other Health data visualized through metrics and trends of any given day

◎ Insights - Effortlessly assess your Health Vital trends

◎ Heart Rate Alerts - Choose your own high and low heart rate limits, set mute times, and opt to be notified only on sustained changes

◎ HRV Center - Control the quality and context of the Heart Rate Variability data behind your trends

◎ Sleep - Multiple sleep sessions, naps and time out of bed, with your heart rate through the night

◎ Analytics Plus - Gain a unique understanding of important metrics such as Cardio Recovery, Heart Rate Zones and plenty more
