Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

The app is free to download, but you are prompted to pick a subscription plan for premium access to various features and accounts within the app. The app offers a variety of sounds and audios to persuade your mind to see the negatives of smoking, and push for a healthier lifestyle. The app states that it does not guarantee that one could quit smoking with their app, and encourages alternatives alongside their app.
