Blood Pressure monitor is a smarter way to manage your blood pressure measurements and track your progress.

Our free blood pressure monitor in offers you many options to control your blood pressure and pulse. You can simply measure your blood pressure with your blood pressure device and enter the values in the diary.

Blood Pressure monitor allows you to keep track of your readings. You should keep track of your blood pressure, heartbeat, and other vital signs. There's also a section for notes.

With this app you don’t need any paper work or excel sheet You can always carry your medical history in your iphone.

Features:

-You can add your old readings

- Record systolic, diastolic blood pressure, pulse rate .

- Using the note portion, quickly apply notes (e.g. before dinner) and measuring detail (e.g. sitting, left arm).

- Examine your success with the aid of intuitive diagrams and statistics (including mean and variability for a period). Useful for determining how well you're doing and whether any lifestyle changes or drugs have proved to be successful. Based on your notes, compare graphs and statistics.
