Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Great app!!! Love it already. The only criticism is that it seems to be designed to require many habits to keep the rabbit's health bar full. I wanted to start small and entered only two habits, but was forced to add more habits because otherwise my health drains faster than I can refill it. I checked off my habits every day but still got to 0 health.

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Your productivity pet is here to help you stick to good habits!

Your rabbit's home becomes more clean and colorful the more you complete your habits.

Earn carrots to customize your rabbit and design its house.

View other people's Habit Rabbit on the leaderboard!

Your rabbit will...

- give you daily motivation quotes or productivity tips

- ask you how you're feeling and track your moods

- give recommendations on what habit to work on

- cheer you on every time you complete a habit
