Tochi: Your All-in-One Mental Health Journal & Mood Tracker App

Tochi is a complete mental health app designed for real-life emotional wellbeing and mental wellness. Whether you need an anxiety journal, a stress journal, or a gratitude journal with guided prompts, Tochi is built for you. From managing depression, bipolar disorder, and ADHD to navigating burnout or daily stress, this is the mental health journal that adapts to your journey.

Effortless Mood Tracker Check-Ins

The Tochi mood tracker makes tracking progress effortless. Simply open the app, pick an emotion, add a trigger, and you’re finished in under a minute. Whether you use it as an emotion log or a gratitude journal, every entry builds the self-awareness necessary for emotional wellbeing. The insights you gain from a consistent mental health journal make lasting mental wellness possible.

CBT Journal Prompts & Guided Reflection

Tochi is a comprehensive mental health journal, not just a basic log. After every mood tracker entry, CBT-inspired guided prompts help you process feelings, manage anxiety, reframe stress, and break unhealthy patterns. By using Tochi as an anxiety journal or a mental health journal for depression and ADHD, you turn raw data into genuine insight.
