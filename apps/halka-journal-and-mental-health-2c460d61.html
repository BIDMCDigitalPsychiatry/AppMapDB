Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Halka is a private mental health journaling app that helps you express yourself freely and honestly without judgment. Words vanish as you type and strokes fade as you draw, so you can release your thoughts and emotions without holding back. When you need a moment of calm, guided breathwork can help you pause and reset. With a disappearing journal, sketchpad, and breathing exercise, Halka gives you space to draw out what you’re feeling.

Halka is designed for privacy. Your content isn't saved, and entries disappear at the end of each session. As a universal app, you can journal across your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Whether it's anxious thoughts you need to write, frustrations you want to draw out, or moments when you simply need to breathe, it's a place to express what you can't elsewhere. Halka is a unique journaling experience that lets you get it out, watch it disappear, and let it go.
