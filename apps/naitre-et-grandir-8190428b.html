Naître et grandir is a French app that supports individuals with content related to wellness and support for pregnant individuals and their children.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Download the Naître et grandir app, the first Quebec app to offer guidance through all the stages of your child’s development, from pregnancy to age 8. This free, ad-free application offers reliable, scientifically validated content tailored to the specific needs of Quebec parents.

After creating your profile, you will have access to:

A real-time pregnancy tracker

Personalized information about every stage of your pregnancy, week by week

Practical information and advice about pregnancy-related symptoms and discomforts

Images that show the development of the fetus and its size, shape, etc. at different stages

A child development tracker you can use to follow your little one’s progress from birth to age 8

Reliable, expert-validated content on pregnancy, child development, behaviour, language skills, motor skills, family life, nutrition, health and well-being, and more

An extensive library of videos, tools, and infographics with answers to all your parenting questions

News and articles about pregnancy, family life, and early childhood

Personalized tracking for each of your children

Unique illustrations created by a Quebec illustrator

A secure backup of your information, in case you lose your device or change phones, plus peace of mind knowing your information will not be used for commercial purposes
