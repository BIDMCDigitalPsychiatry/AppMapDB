Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Sleepa is an easy to use and beautifully designed app that will help you sleep better. Sleepa includes a library of natural and ambient sleep sounds that can be mixed into the perfect relaxing soundscape, along with other simple and powerful features.

Sleepa features include:

Mix, match and save over 120M different combinations of sounds

Immersive, original artwork throughout the app

Home screen widget support, for quick access to your favorite sounds

Dark and light mode support

Ambient alarm and battery-efficient sleep timer

Popular types of white noise & original binaural beats that replicate your brainwaves

Plus, unlock unlimited access to all current and future features with the Better Bedtime subscription.

Sleepa Sound Library:

- Rain sounds: rain light, rain medium, rain thunder, rain under umbrella, rain on roof, rain on the window, rain on leaves, rain water, rain ocean

- Nature sounds: forest, creek, leaves, birds, waterfall, wind, fire, grasshopper, frogs

- City sounds: cars, traffic, rails, subway, airplane, restaurant, keyboard, fan, washing machine

- Relaxation & meditation sounds: piano, flute, stones, bowl, bell, wind chimes, white noise, pink noise, brown noise

- Ambient noise: white noise, pink noise, brown noise

- Binaural beats that replicate your brainwaves: alpha, beta, gamma, delta, and theta waves

Subscription terms for Better Bedtime can be reviewed here: https://maplemedia.io/terms-of-service/
