Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

This app provides users with spaces to input heath data like blood pressure, medication, height and weight and more. This data is summarized into stats and graphs. The app is fully functional without premium, purchasing this version removes ads and allows for multiple user profiles.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

The Blood Pressure Monitor app makes it easy for you to manage your blood pressure, weight, temperature, and other health data. It comes with a range of useful features, including lifetime data tracking, chart visualization, statistics reporting, medication correlation, email import/export, optional cloud data synchronization, reminders, and much more.

One of the standout features of our app is its comprehensive data tracking capabilities. With this app, you can manage all of your health and medical readings in one place, making it easy to manage your health and stay on top of any potential issues. Whether you are trying to maintain a healthy weight, keep your blood pressure under control, or monitor your medication, this app has you covered.

- Comprehensive reading input fields collection

- Customizable input data form

- Simplified user interface

- Optimized keyboard input

Medication correlation - have you ever wanted to find out how your vital signs change according to different medication you take? How your blood pressure differs on two different arms? This app is designed to help you find out.

- Both oral and injection types of medication tracking and correlation

- Left/right arm tracking and correlation for blood pressure measurement

- Body position tracking and correlation for blood pressure measurement

- Meal type tracking and correlation for glucose measurement

- More to come.
