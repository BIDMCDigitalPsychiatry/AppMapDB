Blood Pressure Tracker – Take Control of Your Heart Health Today ❤️

Struggling to keep track of your blood pressure readings?

Blood Pressure Tracker makes it easy to log, monitor, and understand your numbers—so you can take control of your health with confidence.

Whether you're managing hypertension, tracking daily readings, or supporting a loved one, this app gives you the clarity and insights you need.

⚠️ Important: This app does NOT measure blood pressure. It is designed for recording, tracking, and analysis only.
