Find your Healthy. Simple journaling. Smart coaching.

AteMate is a simple health journal with an AI coach built in. The journal captures your day in seconds. The coach helps you understand what it all means.

For years, health apps gave you data and left you to figure it out alone. AteMate closes that gap. Snap a photo, log how you feel, and your AI coach helps you see the patterns you could not see before.

No rules. No guilt. No starting over.

•• A Simple Journal ••

Capture your life in seconds. Food, mood, hydration, movement, and sleep, all in one place. Snap a photo of your meal and it is recognized automatically. No typing, no measuring, no math. The journal is designed to get out of your way.

•• A Smart Coach ••

Your AI Coach has full context of your journal going back months. Ask it anything.

"Why do I crash every afternoon?"

"Am I eating enough protein this week?"

"How do I recover after a big weekend?"

It sees the patterns you cannot and helps you understand what to do next. It will not give medical advice. Think of it as a knowledgeable companion that knows your full health history and is available whenever you need it.

Note: If you work with a real health coach through AteMate, your experience is built around them, not the AI coach.
