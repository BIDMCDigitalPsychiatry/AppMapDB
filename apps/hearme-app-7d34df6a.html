Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

App allows users to connect to a peer to chat with in real time. Users will then journal after chatting with the peer specialist. The app does require an active subscription for any meaningful engagement, although it is free to download and has a free week trial.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

HearMe is an on-demand emotional wellness platform that connects you with real people for private, real-time support. You can chat with peer Listeners, engage with trained Guides, and access licensed Clinicians where available. The app also includes AI-guided reflections and Sleep Insights to help you understand how your rest connects to your daily emotional experience. Whether you need to talk through a tough moment or build long-term resilience, HearMe provides a safe, structured space to reflect, connect, and grow.
