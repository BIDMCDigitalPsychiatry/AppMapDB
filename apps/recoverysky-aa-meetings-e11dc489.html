Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Recovery is hard enough. Finding a meeting shouldn’t be.

RecoverySky helps you find live online recovery meetings across AA, NA, and Recovery Dharma — all in one beautiful, intuitive app. No Zoom app download. No Zoom account. Just open RecoverySky, tap a meeting, and join.

WHAT’S LIVE RIGHT NOW

Most meeting apps give you a schedule. RecoverySky shows you what’s happening right now.

Open the app and instantly see live meetings at this moment — sorted, searchable, and one tap away. Found a meeting you love? View its full weekly schedule, including every recurring session, not just today’s listing.

When you need a meeting, you need it now. RecoverySky is built around that truth.
