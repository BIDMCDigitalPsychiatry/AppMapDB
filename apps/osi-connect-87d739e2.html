Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

OSI Connect is an app for Canadian users who may struggle with mental health concerns such as PTSD, anxiety, and depression, and provides them with information and nearby resources. The app is completely free to download and to use.

From the app store

Description from the app store — not vetted by MIND

Developed at The Royal in partnership with Veterans Affairs Canada, is full of helpful tools, such as self-assessments, videos of people telling their own stories of recovery from OSIs, advice for families and loved ones of people with an OSI, self care strategies, and resources for healthcare providers.
