Rater review

This review represents the view of the app rater and is not an endorsement by MIND.

Relay is an app aimed at providing a chat based support system to users struggling with drug or alcohol addiction, or food addiction such as binge eating disorder. The app places users into teams based on the problem they are struggling with and gives points for reaching out or chatting with the group. The app also has daily surveys that asks questions about mood, motivation to change and strategies that have worked in the past. Users set a daily goal for their mental health such as "call a friend three times a week" and they receive points for completing this goal. Additionally the app tracks engagement streaks. The app also has a "red flag" feature that allows users to indicate when they are feeling overwhelmed, stressed, depressed, lonely or triggered. The app then notifies other team members and encourages them to reach out to the user in crisis. However, the app does not indicate that the user should seek emergency help or indicate that the app is not a replacement for medical care. Also, the app does not screen messages sent in the chat and therefore, harmful messages could be sent without oversight. The app also allows progress to be shared with friends and the user can choose how much is shared.
