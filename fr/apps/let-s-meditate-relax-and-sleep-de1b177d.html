With over 3 million downloads and a decade of trust, Let's Meditate is the no-nonsense, completely free guided meditation app for anyone looking to reduce stress, sleep better, ease anxiety, and build a mindfulness practice — without the pressure, clutter, or cost.

✦✦✦

🧘 MEDITATIONS FOR EVERY MOMENT

Choose from a large collection of Guided Meditations. Whether you have 5 minutes or 30, there's always a session waiting for you.

• Sleep meditations to quiet a racing mind before bed

• Anxiety & stress relief for moments of panic

• Morning meditations to start your day with intention

• Focus & clarity sessions for midday resets

• Body scan & breathing exercisesfor deep relaxation

The library also includes sleep stories, nighttime rituals, unguided melodies, and more.

✦✦✦

❤️ WHAT MAKES LET'S MEDITATE DIFFERENT

Free — genuinely. Every meditation in the library has always been free. There are no locked tracks hiding behind a paywall.

No ads. Ads and relaxation don't go together. There have never been any, and there never will be.

Respectful notifications. We never spam. If you keep dismissing the daily reminder, the app quietly stops sending it. We would rather you come back on your own terms.

Offline listening. Download only the tracks you want to save; listen to them without an internet connection whenever you like.

Pin a track to your homescreen. If you have a go-to meditation, you can add it as a shortcut and launch straight into it without opening the app.
