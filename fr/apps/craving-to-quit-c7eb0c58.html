Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Ready to quit smoking, vaping, or tobacco? Craving to Quit® is a clinically proven tobacco cessation program that helps you break free from nicotine addiction and take back your health.

Through guided video lessons, mindfulness practices, chat-based coaching, and progress tracking, you’ll learn step by step how to reduce cravings, manage withdrawal symptoms, and finally quit tobacco for good. Whether your goal is to stop smoking cigarettes, quit vaping, or give up smokeless tobacco (dip, chew), Craving to Quit gives you the tools and support to succeed.

Craving to Quit is based on a smoking cessation program developed and tested at Yale University. In clinical trials, this approach proved twice as effective as the gold standard treatment. The 21-module program helps you address both the physical and psychological sides of nicotine addiction, including cravings, withdrawal, stress triggers, and habit loops.
