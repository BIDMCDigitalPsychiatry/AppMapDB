About this app

Drs Dobbin & Ross’s relaxing audio app that has helped 100,000+ people.

Feel Better, Faster – with Sorted and Proven Positive Mental Training

The Sorted mental health app (formerly the Feeling good app) is used and accredited by the NHS - a mark of its effectiveness, safety and good function. The app is very easy to use, just plug in your earphones and play the audios to start feeling the positive benefits.

Discover the power of Positive Mental Training, a scientifically proven approach combining deep relaxation and goal-focused visualisation. Rooted in the latest neuroscience and Olympic sports coaching techniques, our app helps you build mental strength, resilience, and positivity – so you can thrive, not just cope.

A Unique, Upstream CBT Approach

Unlike traditional CBT that targets negative thinking, our upstream CBT (uCBT) method focuses on increasing positive emotions first, which in turn transforms thoughts and behaviours. By harnessing applied relaxation and powerful visualisations, you’ll experience meaningful, lasting change.

Personalised Goal-Oriented Modules

Our app features goal-based audio modules to help you quickly find what you need:

● Calm & Relax: Foundations to develop deep mind/body relaxation

● Improve Mood: to build resilience

● Resolve Depression

● Ease Anxiety & Worries

● Work/Life Balance: Relax,

● Motivation & Confidence to Improve performance.
