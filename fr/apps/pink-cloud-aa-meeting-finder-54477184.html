Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

This app is used to help users book online and local AA meetings (using geolocator and a calendar format), track attendance and track notes in a sobriety journal. Users can also select which activities they have completed from a list (such as reaching out to a Sponsor or meditating) or create their own. This app offers 4 prayers, as well as motivational texts, aimed towards Christian users.

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Comprehensive recovery support for 19 addiction types. Find AA, NA, CMA, Al-Anon, and SMART Recovery program meetings instantly. Track your sobriety journey privately.

Pink Cloud is a recovery app supporting multiple addiction types across 5 established recovery programs. Whether you're dealing with alcohol, gambling, gaming, or any other challenge, we're here to help.

19 ADDICTION TYPES SUPPORTED

Substances: • Alcohol • Meth & Heroin • Cocaine • Marijuana • Pills & Prescriptions • Nicotine & Vaping

Behavioral Addictions: • Gambling • Gaming • Internet & Technology • Social Media • Sex & Porn • Food & Eating • Shopping • Work & Workaholism

Life Challenges: • Relationships • Trauma • Family Issues

5 RECOVERY PROGRAMS

Access meetings and resources for:

• Alcoholics Anonymous (AA)

• Narcotics Anonymous (NA)

• Crystal Meth Anonymous (CMA)

• Al-Anon Groups

• SMART Recovery (CBT-based)

KEY FEATURES

Meeting Finder: • 180,000+ meetings worldwide • Real-time search by location • Filter by day, time, type • Turn-by-turn directions • Online/Zoom meeting links • Save favorite meetings • Accessibility information • Special focus meetings (Women's, Men's, LGBTQ+)

Sobriety Tracker: • Multiple addiction counters • Daily motivation • Private journal • Meeting attendance log • Step work tracker • No account required

Privacy Protected: • 100% anonymous usage • No email required • Local data storage on your phone • You control your information.
