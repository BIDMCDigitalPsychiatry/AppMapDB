HOW EATING DISORDER RECOVERY WORKS

- Track your feelings

- Track your symptoms

- Track your treatments

- Add your own symptoms

- Add your own treatments

- Add notes

- Have an easy overview on how you are doing

- Analyze your statistics over time

- Create PDF reports and share them with your doctor

- Create CSV reports and share them with your doctor

- Collect new achievements by using the app

- Stay on track with daily reminders

- Lock your journal with Face Id or Touch Id
