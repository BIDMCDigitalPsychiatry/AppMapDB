Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

This app provides users with "to-dos", tracking symptoms and mood, as well as challenges which allow them to work towards their goals (e.g. mediating daily for 10 mins). However, these mood and symptom trackers do not keep data over time meaning that users can only see the responses they have just inputted and not a summary over time. The only data that is summarized over time is from the challenges. Received an instant response on the chat feature from a counsellor who provided resources for therapy and asked if there were any specific that goals I wanted to explore.
