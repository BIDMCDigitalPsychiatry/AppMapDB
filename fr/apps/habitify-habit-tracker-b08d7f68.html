Building a healthy, disciplined life shouldn’t feel overwhelming. From daily habits and occasional schedules to breaking addiction—track them all in one place.

Design your path to wellness with Habitify, whether you're aiming to build healthy habits or quit bad routines. By focusing on lasting small actions that deliver huge life values—like taking a short walk, drinking 32oz of water, or making your morning bed—Habitify is the only goal tracker you need. You'll have access to an intuitive task reminder, a powerful focus timer, beautifully visualized data insights, and seamless cross-device syncing. You’ll soon discover why our 3M+ users maintain incredible habit streaks and finally achieve the wellbeing they desire.

Habitify is more than a simple to-do checklist; monitor your progress and reflect on your day with our science-backed system. Here’s how we help you lock in a new lifestyle for both body and mind:

• Organize your life - effortlessly track habits, routines, and personal goals. Group them by time of day (morning, afternoon, evening) or use custom areas to keep your daily schedule crystal clear (e.g., meal planner, home workout).

• Nurture your health - record runs, track steps, log water intake, monitor sleep, and manage stress with meditation.

• Build routines that stick - use time and location-based smart reminders so your self-care becomes second nature. You can even use habit stacking—a scientifically proven strategy that helps your brain learn discipline…
