Help Your Child Drift Into Peaceful Sleep

Struggling with bedtime, worries, or night-time restlessness? Children’s Sleep Meditations gently guides your child into deep relaxation with beautifully crafted bedtime stories and calming meditations, designed to help them feel safe, calm, and ready for sleep.

Created by trusted children’s wellbeing experts and bestselling meditation authors, this app supports healthy sleep habits and emotional balance for children of all ages.

Soothing Bedtime Sleep Meditations

Calm, reassuring voices guide children into a relaxed state, helping their mind and body naturally unwind before sleep.

Magical Sleep Stories & Mindfulness

Imaginative story meditations that capture attention while gently easing children into rest.

Support for Anxiety, Confidence & Calm

Sessions designed to help with worries, school stress, self-esteem, emotional regulation, and feeling safe at night.

Simple, guided breathing prompts that teach children how to relax their body and slow their thoughts.

Professionally Produced Audio

High-quality recordings with dreamy music and gentle soundscapes for a comforting bedtime atmosphere.
