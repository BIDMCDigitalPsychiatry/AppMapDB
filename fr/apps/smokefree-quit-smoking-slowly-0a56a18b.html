Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

User-friendly app to track smoking habits. Additional features include a personalised plan to meet goals set for smoking cessation with ability to track where and why smoking occurs for the app user. Quotes and images optional to include to increase motivation. Accessibility features include options to shift between dark, light and automatic modes, with option to change colour and widget themes. Free app with various payment options, including ad removal.

Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

SmokeFree app is the tool you need when trying to quit smoking. You will slowly reduce the amount of cigarettes per day, keep your cessation stress and nicotine cravings in normal and let you body adapt to less cigarettes daily, until you completely stop. But still, the most important thing is your desire to stop smoking.

Why should you try this method?

This app provides method that is different from just saying "I won’t smoke anymore". That’s really hard. Reduction of nicotine and stress from no-smoking are coming in small doses here.

Remember: Desire to quit smoking is most important. You can do it! :-)

Features

• Create your own personalized quitting plan

• Automated quitting method will adapt to your smoking habits and move quitting date if it gets too hard

• Track your daily progress

• Get notified when you are allowed to smoke

• Add quotes and images as motivations

• Data synchronization between all your devices
