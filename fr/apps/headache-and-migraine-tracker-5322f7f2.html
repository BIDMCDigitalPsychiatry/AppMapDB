Take Control of Your Headaches and Migraines with Headache Tracker

Manage your headache and migraine symptoms effectively with our headache and migraine tracking app.

Whether it's stress, caffeine, exercise, or other triggers, this symptom tracker helps you log and analyze pain, aiding communication with your healthcare provider and identifying the root causes of your migraines or cluster headaches.

Key Features:

- Easy Logging: Record headache details like duration, severity, pain location, pain type, and triggers.

- Pain Locations: Identify headache types such as TMJ, Migraine, Tension headache, Cluster headache, and sinus pain.

- Calendar View: Visualize your migraine history with a detailed calendar.

- Symptom Analysis: Free basic analysis to track and identify patterns.

- Premium Features: Advanced analysis, detailed graphs, and medication tracking to correlate medication use with headache patterns.

Headache Tracker is more than a migraine journal; it’s a comprehensive symptom tracker, pain analysis, and reporting tool. Log your headache and migraine traits, analyze symptoms, and easily share this data with your healthcare provider for better management and treatment.
