| Designed by LGBTQIA+ Therapists |

| Supporting 50,000+ Queer People Globally |

| Featured by Apple for Pride & App of the Day in 35+ Countries |

Most mental wellbeing apps weren't built for queer lives. Voda is. Whether you're carrying burnout at work, stress, coming out worries, family conflict, a breakup, or the slow loneliness of not being fully seen — Voda is a private space designed around how queer people actually live.

YOUR PERSONALISED 10-DAY JOURNEY

Get a personalised 10-day journey with wellbeing practices drawn from approaches like ACT, CFT, CBT, DBT and IFS, tailored to what you're actually carrying right now.

Designed for the realities of queer life:

- Carrying minority stress

- Self-worth in unaffirming environments

- Coming out — at any age or stage

- Navigating the realities of transition

- Burnout, identity, and disclosure at work

- Repair after rejection, shame, and isolation

1-MINUTE DAILY WISDOM

Feels like a horoscope. Written by queer therapists. A 1-minute insight every morning, tailored to your needs.

5-MINUTE DAILY PRACTICES

Open the app, get a wellbeing practice you can do in five minutes. Made for the days you have capacity. And the days you don't.

QUEER MEDITATIONS

Meditations voiced by LGBTQIA+ creators. Find calm before a hard conversation, soften a triggering moment, and reconnect with your body.
