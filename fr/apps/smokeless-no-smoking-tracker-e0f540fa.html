Smokeless: Master Your Smoking Habits Today!

Join thousands of users transforming their lives with Smokeless, the ultimate cigarette tracker app. Whether you aim to quit smoking, reduce your cigarette use, or track your habits, Smokeless delivers powerful tools and instant insights to guide you. See results from day one. Download now to start your journey to a healthier you!

Why Smokeless Stands Out

Smokeless empowers you in three powerful ways:

Quit Smoking Now: Personalized plans help you stop tobacco use with clear, step-by-step guidance for smoke cessation.

Reduce Smoking: Lessen cigarette use at your own pace with strategies tailored to cut down gradually.

Track Your Smoking: Monitor daily cigarette habits with an intuitive tracker that reveals your patterns instantly.

Core Features

Precise Cigarette Tracker: Log every cigarette with one tap for an accurate record of your smoking habits.

Advanced Analytics: Gain clarity with daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly charts that highlight trends and triggers.

Smart Quit Programs: Choose from three proven methods:

• Linear Descending: Reduce cigarettes step-by-step.

• Fixed Interval: Space out smoking with a structured schedule.

• Minimum Interval: Increase gaps between cigarettes for mindful habits.

Motivational Timer: Track time between cigarettes with a built-in stopwatch, encouraging longer breaks.
