Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Twilight offers resources such as psychoeducation, journals and relaxation games for users to improve their mental health. The app also contains multiple crisis call and text lines for times of emergency. The app is completely free to download and to use.

This app was a winner of the WWDC 2022 Swift Student Challenge!

Be yourself again.

Use Twilight to find mental health professionals like psychiatrists, therapists, and counselors around you. Don't feel comfortable seeing someone in person? Access free services from national and state crisis hotlines.
