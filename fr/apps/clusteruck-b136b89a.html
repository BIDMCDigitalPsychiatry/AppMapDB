ClusterUck: Comprehensive Solution for Headache Tracking

ClusterUck offers a complete, user-friendly solution for recording and managing cluster headaches and other headache conditions. Designed for those who experience recurring headaches, ClusterUck enables detailed logging and precise monitoring of each episode.

• Detailed Tracking: Record essential information for each headache episode:

• Attack Duration

• Pain Location (specific areas such as temples, forehead, etc.)

• Potential Triggers (e.g., food, stress, sleep patterns)

• Symptoms (such as nausea, sensitivity to light/sound)

• Medications Taken (type, dosage, and effectiveness)

• Weather Data (for insights on weather-related factors)

• Data Analysis and Insights: Analyze your headache patterns with clear, easy-to-read graphs that provide insights into attack duration, severity, and frequency over time.

• Export and Share Options: Easily share your headache records with a healthcare provider by exporting reports in PDF or CSV format, enabling quick and effective professional review.

With ClusterUck, you have the tools to capture critical information for improved headache management.
