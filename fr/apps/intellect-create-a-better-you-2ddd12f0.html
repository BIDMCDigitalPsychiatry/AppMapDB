Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Intellect is a self-managed mental wellness app that focuses on helping users develop healthier habits and behaviors, and progress towards personal goals, such as improving social skills, managing stress, or improving work performance. Users can choose to address stress, self-esteem, assertiveness, and emotional wellbeing, with sessions designed to be completed over several weeks.

The app is free to download but offers paid Premium subscriptions, including a $49.99 annual subscription in the U.S. App Store.

Features

One of Google’s Best Apps of 2020, Intellect serves over 50 countries worldwide. Intellect isn’t just your average app for on-the-go therapy. The app features a range of self-guided cognitive behavioural therapy (cbt) programs to help users navigate through everyday challenges like procrastination, managing stress and relationship issues.

For enterprise users as well as consumers in select markets, the app also offers a matching system to find a therapist or behavioural health coach, specially certified by Intellect, to cater to your unique needs.
