Your brain runs on rhythms. Delta waves for deep sleep. Beta waves for sharp focus. Alpha waves for calm. Theta for inner exploration. Gamma for peak cognition. When your brainwaves fall out of sync, sleep slips away, focus fades, and mental noise takes over.

**Brainwaves** is an immersive audio experience using binaural beats to guide your brain toward the state you need — deep sleep, sharp focus, calm, or recovery. No meditation required. Just headphones and 15–60 minutes.

### Focus Aid & Brain Fog

Cut through mental fog with beta-wave sessions (13–30Hz). Sharpen clarity for deep work, study, or creative projects. Perfect for post-retirement cognitive maintenance or daily productivity.

### Anxiety Calm & Stress Relief

Quiet the noise in your head. Alpha-theta soundscapes (4–12Hz) dissolve tension and bring your nervous system back into balance — no meditation experience required.

### Health & Recovery

Solfeggio frequencies combined with brainwave audio for physical balance, whole-body reset, and chronic fatigue support. Drug-free alternative for rest and restoration.
