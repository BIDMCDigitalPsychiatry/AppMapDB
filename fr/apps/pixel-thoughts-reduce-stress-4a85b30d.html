Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Pixel thoughts is an app that allows users to engage in meditation and mindfulness when they're feeling stressed. Users type a stressful thought in a star as the app guides the user on a meditation exercise.

Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Pixel Thoughts is a spiritual successor to the popular Pixel Thoughts web.

Take a brief moment out of your day to enjoy a beautiful meditation on the relationship between our self and the universe. Simply focus on something that is troubling you and let the meditation offer you some perspective.

Recommended with headphones.

We hope you enjoy and feel a little more relaxed.
