Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Simple app that tracks the frequency of cigarette use. Users can see how many cigarettes they have smoked over periods of time and set goals to reduce the use of cigarettes. Frequency of adds in the app can be intrusive.
