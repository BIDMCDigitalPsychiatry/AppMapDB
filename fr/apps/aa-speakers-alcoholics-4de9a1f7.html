Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

This is a simple app that allows users to listen to AA testimonials in the app. The app categorizes these speeches and allows users to favorite and download the speeches for offline use. There is a paid version of the app that does not have ads.

Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

The AA speakers app allows you to listen to recovery speakers, workshops and audio books from Alcoholics Anonymous (a 12 step program) whenever and wherever using simply a wifi or cellular network connection.

The app includes

600+ speakers speaking at Alcoholics Anonymous conventions worldwide with descriptions of where they are from and where they are speaking.

The Joe and Charlie Big Book Study - a series of tapes talking about the Big Book of Alcoholics Anonymous.

The Big Book of Alcoholics Anonymous Audio Book.

The 12 Steps and 12 Traditions of Alcoholics Anonymous Audio Book

This app is perfect for when you cant get to a meeting, take recovery with you wherever you go!

The content of this app is not being sold merely the software that plays it.
