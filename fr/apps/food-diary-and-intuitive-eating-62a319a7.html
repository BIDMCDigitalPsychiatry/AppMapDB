Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Munch serves as an intuitive eating food diary. The app states that its objective is to help people with an unhealthy relationship with food and their body image get away from calorie counting and move towards intuitive eating. The app interface allows users to record their meals by uploading a picture of their meal and then completing a reflection. The reflection asks about mood before the meal, hunger before the meal and hunger after the meal.

A SIMPLE FOOD DIARY FOR INTUITIVE EATING

My Food Diary is a simple food diary that helps you see how you eat without calorie counting, rigid rules, or guilt.

Log meals as you eat right now, notice patterns over time, and build a healthier relationship with food using intuitive eating principles.

WHY MY FOOD DIARY

Most food diary apps focus on restriction.

Munch focuses on awareness.

Use My Food Diary as a food diary, free food journal, or easy diet diary to support healthy eating your way.

FEATURES

- Simple food diary with no calories or macros

- Flexible food journal for everyday eating

- See how you eat and understand patterns

- Designed for intuitive eating, not dieting

- Supports healthy eating without pressure

- Easy diet diary that fits real life

- Clean, calm design with no streaks or guilt

WHO ITS FOR

- My Food Diary is for you if you want to

- Use a food diary without calorie counting

- Track meals as you eat right now

- Build healthy eating habits gradually

- Understand hunger, fullness, and routines

- Use a free food journal that feels supportive
