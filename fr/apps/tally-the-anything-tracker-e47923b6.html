Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

TALLY is a simple tool to help you track absolutely anything.

Some uses for TALLY:

• Tracking habits

• Tracking medications / vitamins

• Daily planner / to-do list

• Tracking daily/weekly/monthly spending

• Budgeting or saving money

• Pain journal

• Symptom tracking

• Mood tracking

• Finding patterns in your life

Whether you’re trying to track how many times you ate vegetables this week, how many cups of coffee you drank, or whether or not you took your vitamins today, TALLY can help you get where you want to go.

With its simple and encouraging interface, logging activity is a delight. Each TALLY box will tell you the current tracker count and how long it's been since you last logged activity.

Each tracker is fully customizable. Set your tracker to reset every day, week, month, year or never. Use TALLY's charts, calendars, and notes to analyze activity and detect any progress or patterns. The main screen will display all of your trackers. You can use a filter to display your trackers in a way that makes sense to you.

TALLY is FREE for up to 3 trackers. For more than 3, upgrade to the Premium Version or subscribe to the Growth Bundle.

TALLY Features:

• Quick logging with a Widget and Apple Watch app

• Enter custom values

• Tracking on a daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or never basis

• Easily view progress with statistics

• Multiple reminders for each tracker

• Fun color options let you customize your list

• Easy to edit activities in the past

• Backup to Dropbox and…
