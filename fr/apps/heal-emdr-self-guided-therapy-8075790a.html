Heal EMDR puts clinically proven Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) therapy in your pocket, so you can reduce PTSD, trauma, anxiety, depression and more, anytime, anywhere.

Backed by research and trusted by the WHO, APA, U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, SAMHSA and the UK’s NICE, EMDR has helped millions process distressing memories and reclaim their lives. Heal brings the same evidence-based method to you in simple, guided steps.

Key Features

- Optional AI Therapist or Standard Questionnaire: choose how you want to be guided

- Targeted programs: Beat Anxiety, Conquer PTSD, Heal Trauma, Lift Depression, Cope With Grief, Ease Phobias

- Personalized sessions: adjust tone speed, therapist voice, session length and set count

- Progress dashboard: watch your disturbance level drop, earn streaks and track total therapy time

- Resource library: videos, tips and articles to deepen your understanding of EMDR

- 100% private: all data stays on your device; nothing is shared or sold

Why EMDR With Heal

- Faster relief than many talk-therapy methods

- No need to relive every detail of traumatic events

- Proven to reduce anxiety, depression and negative self-beliefs

- Affordable, unlimited access - costs less than a single in-person session

- Start immediately; no waiting lists
