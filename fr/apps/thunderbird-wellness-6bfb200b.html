Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Thunderbird wellness provides Indigenous youth with information regarding substance abuse, addiction, and mental health issues. The app also includes surveys regarding substance use which users can fill out in order to help communities.

Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Thunderbird Wellness is an app featuring emotional intelligence based tools, to help identify and understand emotions, as well as activities to help manage emotions, such as meditations, breathing exercises, beading exercises, affirmations, strength finding activities, and more!
