Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

CardioVisual is a health education tool developed by clinicians to help improve patient education. Helping patients understand their health conditions and treatments is critical to ensuring they engage in their own care and achieve better health outcomes. But finding quality, accurate health education resources can be challenging.

Clinicians are busy and have limited tools and resources to give patients a robust educational experience, and patients can be overwhelmed by hundreds of thousands of search results. CardioVisual offers the most trusted videos and playlists providing comprehensive information, so patients and clinicians can feel confident in their patient education program.

CardioVisual has partnered with top healthcare experts and companies in the world to curate nearly 500 pieces of clinician-approved, high-quality, up-to-date health education content.

CLINICIANS can:

• Create custom video playlists

• Share videos, playlists and calculators with patients

• Download videos for use anytime, anywhere

• Watch practical education videos from experts and medical companies

• Easily integrate into the daily workflow for a busy clinic or hospital

PATIENTS can:

• Watch content shared by their clinician and save time from searching

• Discover new and relevant content to continue their health education

• Access calculators and other tools to better understand health risks

• Share content with family & caregivers

• Take quizzes to reinforce learning
