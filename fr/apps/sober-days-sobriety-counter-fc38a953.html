Sober: Your Path to Freedom and Self-Discovery.

Sober helps you quit or reduce harmful habits — from alcohol and smoking to drugs, self-harm, junk food and beyond. It’s more than a tracker — it’s a daily source of motivation, reflection, and community support. Wherever you are in your journey, Sober helps you take the next step with confidence and compassion.

Free Features

• Customizable Counter: Track your progress your way — by days, milestones, or moments that matter.

• Custom Wallpapers: Personalize your recovery space to stay inspired every time you open the app.

• Year Overview: See how far you’ve come and celebrate every small victory.

• Motivational Quotes: Daily words of encouragement to keep your spirit strong.

• History of Records: Reflect on your growth and patterns over time.

• Hangover Advice: Practical tips for healthier days and smarter choices.

• Recovery Timeline: Visualize your healing journey from day one to long-term success.

• Anonymous Community Journal: Share your thoughts, victories, and struggles — connect with people who truly understand.

• Social Sharing: Celebrate milestones with beautifully designed posts you can customize and share.

Premium Features

Unlock even more ways to stay motivated and mindful:

• Home Widgets: Keep your counter and quotes visible for constant encouragement.

• Multiple Counters: Track progress for different habits simultaneously.

• Achievements: Earn rewards for milestones that remind you how far you’ve come.
