Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Folia Health is an app that allows for users to manage their help and support their care provided by medical professionals. It allows users to track symptoms and spot patterns in terms of both physical and mental health.

Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Chronic illness often feels lonely, and rarely fits in a box. Join 40,000+ patients and caregivers who use Folia to make sense of it all.

Built for patients and caregivers, Folia aims to close the gap between what researchers know and what patients experience.

• GET PAID FOR PARTICIPATING IN RESEARCH - through Data Dividends, if you're opted in

• COMPLETELY FREE TO USE - no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no hidden costs

• CUSTOMIZED TO YOU - track what you want, on your terms

• BUILT BY PATIENTS & CAREGIVERS - so we know what actually matters

• FEEL PREPARED FOR YOUR NEXT APPOINTMENT - answer "how are you feeling?" with reports & insights in 10 seconds

TRACK WHAT MATTERS TO YOU

Health is personal. Your tracker should be too. Capture what you want, how you want:

• Symptoms on a 0-5 scale, yes/no, count, or custom tags

• Medications on a schedule or as-needed (PRN)

• Flare-ups in real time, with triggers and treatments

• Mental health, pain, sleep, nutrition - your way

• Caregiver mode for tracking on behalf of someone you love

SPOT PATTERNS. GET ANSWERS.

Folia turns daily entries into clear graphs and reports:

• Identify triggers that worsen your symptoms

• Find the habits and treatments that actually help

• Understand medication side effects over time

• See clear insights and reports so you're ready for every appointment
