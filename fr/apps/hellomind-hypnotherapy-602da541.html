Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

The app provides guided meditation/hypnotherapy sessions in the following categories: Love, Habits , Weight, Sleep, Self-Esteem, Motivation, Stress, Anxiety, Pregnancy, Boosters, and Quick Fixes.

Within these categories, you can further specify what the problem is and receive tailored videos/audios.
