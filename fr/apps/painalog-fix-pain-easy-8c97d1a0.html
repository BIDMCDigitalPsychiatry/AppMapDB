Painalog is an innovative app that puts proven pain-relieving powers right at your fingertips. By combining the time-tested techniques from the world of massage -- trigger point therapy, with customized stretching Painalog can help you locate, diagnose, massage & relieve painful knots within your muscles. The days of being dependent on pain medications, expensive and dangerous surgeries, or feeling like you'll never find relief from muscle pain are over.

1. Mark your pain areas in 3D. Rotate, move, zoom the model. Match your pain pattern against the Human Myofascial Pain Atlas.

2. Check your symptoms against a database of 200+ related conditions.

3. Painalog scans its database to identify the best possible matches for the cause of your pain.

4. Fix it!: Get custom solutions for each muscle/Trigger Point combination to help you get freedom from pain.
