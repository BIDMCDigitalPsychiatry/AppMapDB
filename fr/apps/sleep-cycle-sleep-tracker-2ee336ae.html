Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Start every day right with Sleep Cycle’s intelligent alarm clock. It wakes you gently during your lightest sleep phase, helping you feel energized. Get a detailed analysis of your sleep patterns, detect sounds like snoring and coughing, and enjoy premium features like relaxing sleep sounds, a sleep story library, and more!

3 REASONS YOU’LL LOVE SLEEP CYCLE:

1. Wake up feeling refreshed: Achieve your sleep goals by waking up naturally during your lightest sleep phase.

2. Track your progress: Monitor your sleep habits and reach your sleep goals with detailed tracking and analysis.

3. No wearables needed: Simply place your phone on your nightstand, and Sleep Cycle uses sound analysis to track your sleep without a need for additional wearables or gadgets.

START A BETTER SLEEP ROUTINE:

Sleep Cycle is your companion for building lasting, healthy sleep habits that support your everyday life:

1. Track Your Sleep Score: Sleep Cycle gives you a sleep score every morning based on your sleep quality, helping you understand how well you’ve rested and what you can do to improve.

2. Set Sleep Goals: Define your sleep goals and use Sleep Cycle’s insights to achieve them. Whether it’s more deep sleep, better wake-up mood, or consistent sleep patterns, Sleep Cycle guides you along the way.
