Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

DWA: Sobriety Counter allows for users to input their start date of being sober and the app tracks how much time users have been sober for, gamifying the user experience by unlocking new "levels" for sobriety milestones. The app provides a feature, although only available for users who have paid the one time in-app purchase, that allows them to leave a journal comment if they log a reset on their days without alcohol. Summary data of user's sobriety day count is summarized in a text format, providing an average and percentage of sobriety days in a selected time period as well as through a bar graph format.
