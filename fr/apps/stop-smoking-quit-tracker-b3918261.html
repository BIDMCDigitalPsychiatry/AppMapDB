Stop Smoking, the essential healthcare app to help you quit smoking, is here to help you start living a healthier life today. Quitting smoking is the first step to safeguarding your health and improving your quality of life. This app supports your journey by allowing you to track your progress and see the effects of quitting smoking in real-time as you move toward success.

With over 2,000 reviews and more than 200,000 downloads, this is the essential app for anyone who wants to quit smoking.

■ Real-time status updates

Track your health improvements, the money you’ve saved by quitting, and even how much your life expectancy has increased—all in real-time.

■ Boost your success rate by writing a quit journal

Keep detailed records of your quit journey, including the aids you’ve used, smoking habits, and any slip-ups. Tracking these can increase your chances of success.

■ Stay motivated with a variety of notifications

Receive motivational reminders at regular intervals, health updates, and record reminders to keep your willpower strong and help you stay smoke-free.

■ Easily check your progress with a home screen widget

Our free widget allows you to track how long you’ve been smoke-free right from your home screen.

■ Check statistics to stay on track

View weekly and monthly statistics showing the money you’ve saved, the cigarettes you’ve avoided, and your smoking patterns. Keep filling in the green boxes as you continue to make progress!
