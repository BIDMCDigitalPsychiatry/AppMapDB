Take it. Tick it. Stay on track.

TabletTick is a simple medication reminder and tracker designed to help you stay on top of your medication.

Set reminders for the times you need them, keep track of each dose, manage your medication stock and review your history — all in one simple app.

MEDICATION REMINDERS

Set reminders for every day, specific days or every other day. Add multiple times per day for medications you take more than once.

TRACK YOUR DOSES

See what’s due today and mark doses as taken or skipped. If a dose isn’t recorded, TabletTick can show it as missed, giving you a clear medication history to look back on.

MANAGE YOUR MEDICATION STOCK

Keep track of how much medication you have left. Set low-stock alerts and refill reminders to help you know when supplies are running low.

REVIEW YOUR HISTORY

See your taken, skipped and missed doses and review your medication history by date.

CREATE AND SHARE REPORTS

Create PDF or CSV reports of your medication history. View them, save them or share them when you need to.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account required. No ads or analytics. Your medication information stays on your device.

COMPLETELY FREE

TabletTick is completely free to use, with no subscription required.

Simple medication reminders and tracking, without unnecessary complexity.

TabletTick does not provide medical advice. Always follow the instructions provided by your healthcare professional or on your medication label.
