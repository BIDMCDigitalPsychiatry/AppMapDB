Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Behavidence develops machine learning based tools to detect and assist with remote monitoring and management of mental health conditions through passive digital biomarkers. The company was founded by a neuroscientist, neuropsychologist, physician and bioengineer with the passion to improve the lives of millions suffering from psychiatric and neurological disorders.

You can fill in different psychological questionnaires daily and get your calculated scores. We are collecting anonymized behavioral information to develop ways to detect depression, anxiety and ADHD in the future without any questionnaires. So by downloading and using the app, You will be helping millions with mental health disorders.
