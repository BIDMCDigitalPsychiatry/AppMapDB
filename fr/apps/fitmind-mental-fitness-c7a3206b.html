Maximize your focus, vitality, and joy. FitMind is a meditation program backed by neuroscientists and practiced by monks. In a world of chaos, join the mental fitness revolution.

You'll learn to master meditation in 30 days by progressing through a series of guided trainings. The FitMind app will also explain the science and psychology behind the practice and provide daily challenges for integrating it into your everyday life. This is not "McMindfulness," nor is it just another app to clutter your phone. Here's a deep mental training program that can impact the way you perceive the world.

ABOUT FITMIND

FitMind is a professional mental fitness company teaching at Fortune 500 companies, addiction centers, schools, government offices, and other organizations.

We believe that mental fitness is the next health revolution and are passionate about bringing mind training to a wider audience with a secular and scientific method.

UNIQUE FEATURES:

• 30-day advanced meditation training program that systematically trains your mind

• Mental Fitness scoring and charts so you can track your progress as your mind becomes increasingly fit

• Ability to ask questions about your practice in the app

• Scientific explanations of the techniques and how to apply them throughout the day

• "Daily Challenges" that make meditation a lifestyle, rather than just a habit

• New guided trainings and lessons added regularly

• Access to a meditation community, science research, recommended books, retreats,…
