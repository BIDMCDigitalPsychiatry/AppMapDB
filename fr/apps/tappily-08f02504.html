Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Tappily provides users with game-like activities to help users stay organized and improve their mental health. The app offers various self care, productivity, and journaling prompts and features. It is free to download but many features require a subscription.

Self care for your mind. Therapist recommended.

Tappily uses game-like activities to help users stay organized, think positively, and overcome mental health challenges.

No need to keep track of a journal and pen. Tappily is here for you on the go. It’s conveniently made so you can always reflect on your past thoughts, organize tasks, or take note of something good that just happened.

Tappily uses beautiful animations to make your experience as positive and happy as possible. Be prepared to reduce anxiety and stress, create positive perspectives, and incorporate gratitude, self-love and self-care into your daily life.
