Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

BedTyme is an app intended to help users who experience insomnia. The app requires a purchase of $189.99 per month to access the content. Upon registering users are prompted to answers questions about their sleep and potential triggers of not getting sleep such as stress, irregular sleep patterns and a course is tailored for users based on these questions. There is a sleep diary to track sleep and users are given daily readings and plans on how to improve their sleep. The app is based on CBT-i and there is also a chat feature where users can ask questions to the app's sleep coaches. However, you will not be able to fully access this content unless you do the free trial and pay $189.99.
