Effortlessly remember your daily life with the ultimate no-writing journal and mood tracker.

Looking for a daily journal and mood diary that's a joy, not a chore?

Meet DailyBean, the easiest private diary and life log designed to help you capture your day.

Transform your everyday moments into a beautiful visual journal, reflect on your mood, and keep your memories safe without the pressure of a blank page!

THE SIMPLEST JOURNAL YOU'LL EVER KEEP

Faster than a traditional notebook and easier than a bullet journal, DailyBean is an aesthetic journal made for everyone.

Whether you need a quick brain dump or a mental health journal to manage your emotions, it fits perfectly into your routine. It acts as your safe space and private venting journal when you need it, and a wellness habit tracker when you want to focus on self-care and mood tracking.

RECORD YOUR DAY IN SECONDS

No blank pages, no typing required. Logging your life is as simple as a few taps:

• Choose your mood bean to start your daily journal entry and log your mood.

• Tap icons to record your activities, routines, meals, and more.

• Add a quick photo and a short note to create a wholesome journal entry.

PERSONALIZE YOUR AESTHETIC

Journal DailyBean isn't just functional; it’s a beautifully cute journal you’ll love to use:

• 100% Customizable: Build your own custom blocks. Add or remove category blocks to track exactly what matters to you.

• Beautiful Themes: Decorate your kawaii journal with aesthetic themes…
