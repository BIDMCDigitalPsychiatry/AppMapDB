Aetheria: Your Spatial Sanctuary

Escape the flat world. Enter Aetheria.

Aetheria is not just another noise app. It is a high-performance spatial audio engine designed for those who demand more from their mental space. Whether you are deep-lining code, seeking restorative sleep, or reclaiming your focus from ADHD, Aetheria constructs a living, 3D environment that breathes with you.

THE SPATIAL AUDIO DIFFERENCE

• Standard apps play static, two-dimensional loops. Aetheria utilizes a proprietary 360° sound stage.

• Active Spatialization: Use your screen as a canvas. Drag a crackling fire to your left, heavy rain behind you, and wind in the pines above.

• Dynamic Orbit: Enable "Motion" to let sounds slowly rotate around you. This prevents auditory habituation, keeping your brain engaged in a flow state for longer periods.

NEUROSCIENCE, NOT JUST NOISE

We don’t just offer "relaxing sounds." We offer cognitive control. Layer your environment with scientifically calibrated binaural beats to tune your brain state:

• Delta (2.5 Hz): Deep, restorative sleep.

• Theta (6.0 Hz): Meditation & creative visualization.

• Alpha (10 Hz): Relaxed alertness & stress reduction.

• Beta (20 Hz): High-level focus & cognitive performance.

• Gamma (40 Hz): Peak concentration (Pro).

CINEMA-QUALITY SOUND BANK

Every sound in Aetheria is 100% gapless and high-fidelity.
