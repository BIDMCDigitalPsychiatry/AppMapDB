Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

This app is what you need to quit smoking. Set your quit date, track progress and stay healthy being a non-smoker.

App features:

- Statistics

- Non-smoking days calculation

- Money saved

- Time saved

- Expected life regained

- No ads

The app gives you access to edit everything about your smoking experience: quit date, pack price and size, smoking volume, and even begin calculation of non-smoking days again.

Inspired by WHO statistics, the app helps you calculate expected life regained by quitting smoking cigarettes.

If you have a question or a suggestion to improve the app and be adapted to your needs, feel free to contact us at email: nosmokingapp@gmail.com
