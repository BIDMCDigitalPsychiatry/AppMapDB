Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Life is unpredictable and can sometimes feel completely overwhelming. Wouldn’t it be nice if life came with instructions? Unwinding by Sharecare is essentially just that—a “Quick Start” guide for your mind. By spending only a few minutes each day, Unwinding helps you manage everyday stressors and build healthy habits.

With science-based practices and tools, you can be calmer, more aware, more in your body and mind, and most importantly, more focused on the things that matter most to you right now. Unwinding is a powerful mental health digital therapeutic that helps you stay grounded and gives you the support you need with tools at your fingertips when moments of stress arise —right when you need to take a breath and tap back into your curiosity.

Get unlimited access to exclusive, evidence-based mini courses on topics like anxiety, financial stress, procrastination, burnout, and eating to help you understand how your brain works — and how to retrain it — to be less stressed, mentally stronger, and more able to enjoy your life. You'll learn how stress and anxiety drive many of our bad habits and unwanted behaviors, including overeating, procrastination, self-criticism, and more.

Each mini course is paired with specific awareness-building and behavior change techniques that work to retrain your brain, reduce or eliminate unhelpful habits, and teach you how to improve your overall mental wellbeing, no matter what life throws at you.
