Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Steadyline is a bipolar mood tracker and mood journal for logging mood, sleep, energy, medication, and daily context in minutes.

It is built for people who want a clearer record between appointments. Instead of trying to remember weeks of mood changes, sleep shifts, medication context, and daily notes, Steadyline helps you keep everything in one private place.

Track what matters:

• Mood and energy

• Sleep duration and sleep quality

• Medication records

• Daily notes and journal entries

• Context such as stress, routines, symptoms, and habits

Understand your patterns:

Review trends across days, weeks, and months. Steadyline can highlight notable shifts from your own self-reported logs, so you can reflect on what changed and what may be worth discussing with your care team.

Ask SAM:

Use plain-language questions to explore your logs, compare recent patterns, and understand your own tracked data. SAM uses only the information you choose to log and the privacy settings you enable.

Share with your doctor:

Generate a clear summary for psychiatry appointments or care conversations, so your logs are easier to review without rebuilding everything from memory.

Privacy controls:

Your logs are personal. Steadyline gives you control over optional cloud backup and AI features from privacy settings.
