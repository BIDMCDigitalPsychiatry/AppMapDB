Heart feels off? Inconclusive ECG?

Get your ECGs read by certified experts within minutes, for peace of mind. 500,000+ ECGs read so far. Qaly is the leading ECG app for Apple Watch, Fitbit, Kardia, and other devices.

On the Qaly app, get your ECGs reviewed by certified cardiographic technicians, trained to read ECGs for 30+ palpitations and abnormal heart rhythms like Afib, SVT, PVC, PAC, short or prolonged PR interval, short or long QT interval, WPW, and more. Track your heart rate variability (HRV) to monitor stress and recovery.

The Qaly app (formerly ReadMyECG) is built by Stanford cardiologists and engineers, including Dr. Marco Perez, a Stanford Associate Professor of Medicine and Stanford Cardiac Electrophysiologist.

Join 200K+ heart heroes signed-up with the Qaly app today, the only app where human experts analyze and interpret ECGs from your Apple Watch, Fitbit Watch, Withings Watch, KardiaMobile, WHOOP, Garmin or any other device within minutes, day or night.
