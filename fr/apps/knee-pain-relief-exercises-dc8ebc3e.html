Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

Free app with simple pictures to follow. App claims that simple exercises provided will strengthen muscles surrounding knee-joint. However, app does not claim that it exercises have been approved by trained medical professionals. In addition, no timers have been integrated into the exercises to guide the user through recommended reps and rest periods. No audio support to guide user through exercises. Various advertisements throughout exercise sessions could impact overall user experience.

• No equipment needed — perfect for home or office

• Step-by-step guides for safe and effective exercise execution

• Improve knee flexibility, reduce inflammation, and boost joint health naturally

• Track your progress and pain levels over time

• Suitable for post-surgery rehabilitation and injury recovery

• Free to use with no complicated setup

People with knee osteoarthritis or chronic knee pain

Athletes recovering from knee injury or surgery

Seniors seeking gentle exercises to maintain joint health

Anyone wanting to prevent knee pain and stay active
