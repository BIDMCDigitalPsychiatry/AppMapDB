Comment les applications sont évaluées

Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

This app provides a CBT based support to users to help support mental health improvement. This is achieved through guided journals, thought analysis, and mood analysis. This app is pseudo-free, as it is free to download, but accessing any feature requires you to sign up for a 3-day free trial (after which you are automatically charged). Additionally, there are comments from users on the App store saying that this payment scheme is a scam and that they got charged automatically (recommend archiving this app), payment is over $10 monthly if buying a monthly subscription.
