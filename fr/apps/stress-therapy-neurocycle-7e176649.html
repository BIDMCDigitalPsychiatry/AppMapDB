Avis de l’évaluateur

Cet avis reflète le point de vue de l’évaluateur de l’application et ne constitue pas une recommandation de MIND.

The app has a very easy-to-use interface and audio recordings for a wide range of topics. Even though on the App Store page, it is indicated that the app has in-app purchases, the app does not have a function to allow users to buy a subscription, and all of the audio recordings are available for free.

The app is affiliated with Dr. Caroline Leaf's podcasts and 21-day brain detox challenge, which users can learn about here: https://drleaf.com/collections/online-programs.
