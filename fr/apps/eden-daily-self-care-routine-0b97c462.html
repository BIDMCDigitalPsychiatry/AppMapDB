Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

“We do not decide our future. We decide our habits and our habits decide our future.”

Eden is your new habits tracker companion app. It helps you create and maintain your daily habits and goals.

==============

What set it apart?

==============

For each day you complete your tasks, you will grow a flower little by little. As long as you keep your habits, you will grow a garden and fill it with flowers! It is designed to be a relaxing, non-stressful app that encourages you to make small steps everyday and progress at your own pace.

==============

How does it work?

==============

◆ Create your daily habits, tasks or goals you wish to achieve every day.

◆ Each day, if you mark them all as achieved, a plant will flower, step by step.

◆ However, if you don’t achieve your tasks, a part of the flower will fade away.

◆ Each day, your tasks are automatically refreshed (to do again).

◆ As long as you take care of you, you will take care of your garden.

==============

What else?

==============

◆ A simple, clean, minimalist design.

◆ Very simple to use.

◆ Reminder and timer to help you focus.

◆ Widget to put your garden on your home screen.

◆ No fancy graphics or complex statistics. Focus on today and what you want to accomplish.

◆ Images, ambient sounds & music for you to customize your app.

◆ You can use the app freely to grow your first flower. Get the premium version to grow them all.
