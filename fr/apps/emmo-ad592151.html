EMMO is a place where you can record your mood anytime, anywhere, happy or sad, sour or sweet.

This is a small tree hole, listen to all your good and trouble.

This is a small world that only belongs to you, where you can express your emotions with self-made expressions.

EMMO diary allows people to keep a diary in a different way.

Travel in your own small world in the way of self-made expression + text, come to EMMO diary to record the life.

Backup journal supports icloud backup.

[to do] diary can also make a very practical to-do function.

[export] diary and expression can be exported.

[reminder] time reminder can be set to remind you of any beauty

[font] n multi personalized font selection, you can choose your favorite font according to your hobbies.

[theme] the color of full-color theme can be changed at any time according to your mood.

[timeline] when you write a diary, you can insert a timeline to record the different moods of the day.

[calendar] you can see your mental journey of this month on the calendar.

[Polaroid wall] collect photos from diary to form a photo wall.

[time search] supports the entry of time to search the diary written on any day.

[privacy] privacy can support face ID / touch ID password unlocking, and all data are private.

[map] in the EMMO map, you can recall the footprints of the past.

[Apple pencil] you can write your diary by hand or through apple pencil.

[stickers] there are so many beautiful stickers that you can easily make a hand account.
