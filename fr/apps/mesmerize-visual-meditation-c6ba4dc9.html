Clear your mind and relax with a unique audio-visual meditation experience.

Mesmerize combines engaging visuals with relaxing music and expertly crafted guided meditations to give you an all encompassing meditation experience.

BENEFITS

• Relieve Stress

• Lower Anxiety

• Better Sleep

• Overcome Depression

• Enhance Self-Awareness

• Deeper Relaxation

• Reduce Pain

• Overcome Addiction

• Increase Focus

• In fact, there are many more proven benefits to meditation. The impact of a consistent meditation practice can have a profound impact on the overall quality of your life.

FEATURES

• Engaging Visuals; uniquely hypnotic visuals designed to mesmerize you. Control their speed with a pinch gesture.

• Soothing Psycho-Acoustic Music; designed based on clinically validated principles to ease your body into a state of relaxation.

• Guided Meditations & Hypnosis; expertly crafted to help you on a range of topics.

• Nature Sounds and White Noise; including rain, ocean, thunder, train, fan and much more

• Sleepy Stories; doze off quickly with drowsy tales.

• Focus Music; music designed to help you beat distractions and concentrate.

• Visual Breathing; the speed of visuals will automatically adjust to your selected breathing pattern.

• 3D Voice

• Narration speed control

• Audio Fusion; mix outside audio with Mesmerize playback.

• Sleep Timer; set Mesmerize to automatically stop after a period of time.
