iCardio – Smarter Heart Rate Training (Supports Bluetooth sensors, Apple Watch & Apple Health, and now AirPods Pro G3!)

Track, train, and transform your fitness with iCardio, your all-in-one heart rate training companion. Whether you’re walking, running, cycling, or doing your favorite studio workout, iCardio helps you understand your heart, improve performance, and stay motivated.

Train with Precision

iCardio turns your heart rate into a powerful training tool. See your heart rate zones live as you work out, get instant feedback, and optimize every session to reach your goals faster — whether you’re training for endurance, weight loss, or general fitness.

Connected Fitness, Simplified

iCardio integrates seamlessly with Apple Watch and Apple Health, automatically syncing your workouts, calories, and key metrics like:

* Heart Rate & Zones

* Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

* Resting Heart Rate

Use iCardio as your fitness hub to bring together data from all your favorite Health-connected apps and devices — making it easier to track your overall wellness in one place.

Comprehensive Tracking

* GPS route mapping for outdoor workouts

* Steps, calories, cadence, and power tracking

* Custom Fitness Assessments to personalize zones

* Routes & Routines to measure progress over time

Key Features

* Voice Coaching: Real-time cues to keep you on target

* LiveTracking: Share your workout progress in real time

* Goal Setting: Define and track personal fitness goals
