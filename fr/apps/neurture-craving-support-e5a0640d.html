Selon la boutique d’applications

Description tirée de la boutique d’applications — non vérifiée par MIND

Neurture is the #1 app for managing urges and cravings and a Counselor's Choice Award winner, built for the moments when an urge or craving starts to take over. Open the app, choose a quick tool, and get no-shame support for riding out the hardest minutes instead of acting on autopilot.

Whether you are working with food cravings, binge or emotional eating, food noise, pornography, or other adult-content habits, Neurture helps you understand the trigger, pause the pattern, and choose your next step.

WHY PEOPLE USE NEURTURE

CRAVING & URGE TOOLS

* Use breathing, grounding, urge surfing, thought reframing, affirmations, and values-based check-ins

* Get quick support when a craving feels loud, stressful, or automatic

* Build a menu of tools that fits your real life

TRACK PATTERNS

* Log urges, triggers, outcomes, and reflections

* See what tends to happen before and after cravings

* Notice progress without all-or-nothing pressure

CHANGE PATTERNS WITH COMPASSION

* Work with evidence-informed methods from CBT, ACT, mindfulness, self-compassion, and relapse-prevention skills

* Keep going after a slip with practical reflection instead of shame

* Build a plan for food cravings, binge or emotional eating patterns, pornography, adult content, or related urges

PRIVATE, PRACTICAL SUPPORT

* Use Neurture in the moments between conversations, commitments, and quiet self-reflection

* Choose quick tools when you only have a few minutes

* Return to your goals with more clarity and less…
