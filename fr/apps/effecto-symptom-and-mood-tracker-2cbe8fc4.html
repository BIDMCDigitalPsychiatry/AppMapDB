UNDERSTAND YOUR HEALTH WITH DATA-DRIVEN TRACKING

Effecto is a health and mood tracking app that reveals how daily actions affect your well-being. Get personalized insights showing how habits, symptoms, and lifestyle correlate – helping you identify triggers, optimize routines, and control your health.

1. COMPREHENSIVE TRACKING TAILORED TO YOU

Whether you want to manage chronic conditions, track mental health, or simply understand what affects your mood, Effecto adapts to your unique needs.

Track everything that matters in one simple app with quick daily logging designed for real-life use. Effecto helps you:

Discover patterns between symptoms and daily factors

Identify triggers that impact your mood and health

See correlations between medications, sleep, diet, and well-being

Share accurate health data with doctors and therapists

2. DATA-DRIVEN INSIGHTS THAT EMPOWER

Effecto's smart analytics reveal connections you might miss on your own:

Visual reports showing how factors affect each other

Correlation tracking between symptoms, moods, and habits

Pattern recognition over weeks and months

Actionable insights to improve your daily routine

Do this for a few weeks, and you'll know exactly what to do and what to avoid to improve your health – for good.

3. TRACK EVERYTHING THAT MATTERS

Mental wellness practice anywhere – no equipment needed.

Short lessons that fit busy schedules

Beginner-friendly with advanced progressions

Audio lessons: meditations, affirmations, breathing…
